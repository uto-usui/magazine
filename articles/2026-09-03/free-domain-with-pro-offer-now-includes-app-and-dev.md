We recently announced that all [Pro](https://vercel.com/docs/plans/pro-plan) teams can claim [a free domain for one year](https://vercel.com/changelog/free-domain-for-one-year-now-for-all-pro-teams). That offer now includes `.app` and `.dev`, bringing the eligible TLDs to eight:

-   `.app`
    
-   `.dev`
    
-   `.online`
    
-   `.site`
    
-   `.space`
    
-   `.store`
    
-   `.tech`
    
-   `.website`
    

The offer still covers one domain per team, and premium domains are not eligible. After the first year, your domain will [renew automatically](https://vercel.com/docs/domains/working-with-domains/renew-a-domain) at the regular price shown at checkout when you claim it.

Claim yours at [vercel.com/domains](https://vercel.com/domains), and see the [free domain FAQ](https://vercel.com/docs/domains/free-domain-with-pro) for full terms.
