The **overall UX** performance of the Telco sites is “poor,” with only 1 site in the “mediocre” range.

Here are some of the **overarching UX issues**.

On the benchmarked sites, proprietary plan names and technical specifications were presented as **labels without explanation**.

**No site** [explained its industry-specific filters](https://baymard.com/blog/explain-industry-specific-filters) or provided [inline help](https://baymard.com/guidelines/2148-inline-help-in-comparison-tables) for technical specifications within comparison features, and [plan features](https://baymard.com/guidelines/2850-explaining-telco-plan-features) and [specification labels](https://baymard.com/guidelines/840-explaining-and-defining-specification-labels) were also often undefined.

**Comparison was obstructed** at every level, from plans to handsets to network coverage.

[Contract length](https://baymard.com/guidelines/2955-contract-length-for-telco-plan-listings) was **missing** from plan listings, [comparison features](https://baymard.com/guidelines/495-comparison-features-in-listings-for-spec-heavy-products) were largely absent for handsets, every site listed specifications in [inconsistent units and labels](https://baymard.com/guidelines/839-making-specs-consistent-and-uniform), and 4G and 5G coverage were displayed on [separate maps](https://baymard.com/guidelines/2931-displaying-all-coverage-levels).

The commercial terms of a financed device and ongoing service were **routinely unstated**.

[Warranty upgrades](https://baymard.com/guidelines/819-warranty-upgrades-on-product-pages) were missing on all product pages in the benchmark, and [returns](https://baymard.com/guidelines/803-return-policy-discoverability), [shipping cost](https://baymard.com/guidelines/836-where-to-display-free-shipping-information), [delivery timing](https://baymard.com/guidelines/802-where-and-how-to-display-delivery-information), and the context around [discounts](https://baymard.com/guidelines/816-how-to-display-price-related-special-offers-and-discounts) were each missing from a substantial share.

As this benchmark shows, with **no sites** delivering even a “decent” or better overall user experience, there is plenty of room for improvement.
