📢  Elsewhere in the ecosystem

[![](https://res.cloudinary.com/cpress/image/upload/w_1280,e_sharpen:60,q_auto/zycphwmqibxis7axptyu.jpg)](https://cpojer.net/posts/fastest-frontend-tooling)

-   [npm v11.10.0 has been released](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-02-18-npm-bulk-trusted-publishing-config-and-script-security-now-generally-available/) with a new `--min-release-age` feature (not mentioned in the post), a `--allow-git` flag (defaults to `all` but will become `none` in npm 12), and a way for maintainers to add/update trusted publishing configs across multiple packages at once.
    
-   [Electrobun v1](https://blackboard.sh/blog/electrobun-v1/) – think Electron/NeutralinoJS, but for building cross-platform desktop apps on top of the system webview, Bun, and Zig. A big win is app bundle sizes as low as 12MB. [More info here.](https://blackboard.sh/electrobun/docs/)
