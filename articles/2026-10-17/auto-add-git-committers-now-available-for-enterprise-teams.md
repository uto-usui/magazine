Enterprise teams can now automatically add Git committers to their Vercel team without waiting for owner approval. This applies to private repositories connected to the team’s projects.

Choose between two approval modes:

-   **Auto Approval:** Committers who aren’t already team members are automatically added to your team with the Developer role. Their deployments proceed immediately.
    
-   **Manual Approval (default):** Deployments from committers who aren’t already team members are blocked until a team Owner approves their membership.
    

Committers must have a Vercel account linked to their Git provider. Members added through either mode count toward your team seats under your Enterprise agreement.

Choose your approval mode in **Settings > Members >** [**Collaboration**](https://vercel.com/d?to=%2F%5Bteam%5D%2F%7E%2Fsettings%2Fmembers%23collaboration-settings&title=Go+to+collaboration+settings).
