The pressure on UX- and product teams to deliver results faster has never been higher, but the **requirement** for those results to be rooted in actual user research remains non-negotiable.

At Baymard, we developed UX-Ray to allow UX designers and UX researchers to move rapidly but not recklessly, by providing an instant UX analysis that returns **evidence-backed and fully traceable** UX insights with a [95% accuracy rate](https://baymard.com/blog/ai-heuristic-evaluations).

Over the last 4 months, we’ve achieved **a 5x increase** in the depth of instant UX analysis you can perform on your UX designs.

## UX Analysis Now Across 209 UX Parameters

As of February 1st, 2026, UX-Ray can now conduct an instant UX analysis of your ecommerce site across **209 distinct UX parameters/heuristics**.

This is **up from 39** UX parameters in October 2025.

This expansion lets you move from new design frames to a comprehensive, **research-backed UX audit** in minutes rather than weeks.

## Still with a Documented 95% Accuracy Rate

Speed is **irrelevant if** the UX insights do not improve UX or conversions.

Positive results **hinge on** 1) the accuracy of the UX analysis, and 2) the UX insights being fully evidence-based.

**Accuracy:** UX-Ray still maintains a [documented 95% accuracy rate](https://baymard.com/blog/ai-heuristic-evaluations) when compared to human UX experts auditing the same site:

-   This accuracy rate is continuously tested and retested to ensure the highest standards
-   We publish these results directly on our website so stakeholders can verify the documented accuracy for themselves

**UX insights being fully evidence-based:** A key aspect of UX-Ray is that AI is _never used_ to come up with the UX suggestions themselves or tell you how to improve your site:

-   The AI component is strictly limited to mapping your specific UI patterns to Baymard’s existing platform of [700+ UX research guidelines](https://baymard.com/product/ux-best-practice-guidelines) analyzed and developed by human UX experts
    
-   Every UX recommendation is therefore fully traceable and based exclusively on our 200,000+ hours of large-scale UX research, making the results safe for revenue-generating websites
    

## Advanced Use Cases: Prototypes and Competitors

This **enhanced coverage** isn’t just for live ecommerce sites but you can also:

-   Upload images of upcoming designs to get quality assurance before a single line of code is written
-   Scan and analyze direct competitors to identify where they are succeeding or failing against research-backed principles

Beyond scanning B2C and B2B ecommerce designs, there’s also **partial support** for food, travel, SaaS, and telco (full support coming later in 2026)
