As 2025 comes to a close, Baymard is again awarding the companies with the top **1% UX performance** across 19 industries, as well as the top 1% for theme and platform (e.g., Top 1% UX performance in Cart & Checkout on Mobile, etc.).

Again this year, the pool of award candidates includes both public benchmark companies and select UX Audit clients _if_ they performed as well or better than the benchmark sites.

Each of the 400+ ecommerce websites considered for a Baymard Ecommerce UX Award has been manually rated using on average 500+ UX guidelines; a process that requires more than 20,000 hours of UX auditing work (more on [methodology](https://baymard.com/research/methodology#2-benchmarking-methodology)).

## The Top 1% Baymard Ecommerce UX Award Winners for 2025

The 51 winners are selected from among Baymard’s comprehensive [UX benchmark](https://baymard.com/ux-benchmark) and UX Audit client databases and are based on more than 275,000+ weighted UX performance scores.

Only those sites with a top 1% performance or an exceptional UX performance rating are given the award.

In some instances, there are multiple winners within a category because they all have a top 1% UX performance rating.
