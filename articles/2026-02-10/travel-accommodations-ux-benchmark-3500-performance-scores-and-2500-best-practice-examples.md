The **overall UX** performance of the travel accommodation sites and apps range from very “poor” to “decent”, with **the average** in the “poor” to “mediocre” range.

Many travel accommodation sites and apps falter at the very first step by **failing to prioritize** the [booking search feature](https://baymard.com/blog/travel-accommodations-booking-search) on the homepage, often distracting users from their goal of finding a place to stay.

While most sites get the basics right — like visual consistency, pricing visibility, and clear imagery — they **frequently miss** on deeper essentials such as transparent pricing, effective filters, and review functionality, which adds friction to the decision-making process.

No matter the site and app type — OTA, large-brand chain, boutique hotel, or rental — users **depend on** streamlined tools and rich, organized content to confidently search, evaluate, and book their ideal accommodation.

As our latest benchmark shows, with only 1 “decent” performing site and **none** delivering a “good” or better overall user experience, there is plenty of room for improvement.
