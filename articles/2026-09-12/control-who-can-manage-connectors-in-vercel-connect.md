Pro and Enterprise teams can now control who can create and manage [Vercel Connect](https://vercel.com/docs/connect) connectors. Connectors let applications and agents access external services using credentials managed by your team.

Owners can enable this restriction under **Connector Permissions** in [Team Settings](https://vercel.com/d?to=%2F%5Bteam%5D%2F~%2Fsettings%2Fconnect). Once enabled, only people with the Owner role or [Connector Manager](https://vercel.com/docs/rbac/access-roles/extended-permissions#extended-permissions-for-granular-access) extended permission can create or manage connectors.

Browse [all connectors](https://vercel.com/d?to=%2F%5Bteam%5D%2F~%2Fconnect%2Fbrowse), or learn more in the [Vercel Connect documentation](https://vercel.com/docs/connect).
