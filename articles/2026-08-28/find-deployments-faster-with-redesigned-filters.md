The Deployments page now has redesigned filters that make it faster to find a specific deployment.

You can:

-   Apply suggested filters for common searches.
    
-   Type to find an option instead of scrolling through the list.
    
-   Describe what you’re looking for in natural language, and Vercel automatically applies the matching filters.
    

Open any project's [Deployments page](https://vercel.com/d?to=%2F%5Bteam%5D%2F%5Bproject%5D%2Fdeployments&title=Deployments) to try the new filters.
