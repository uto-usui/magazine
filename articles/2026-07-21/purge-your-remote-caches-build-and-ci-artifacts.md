Team Owners can now clear the team's Remote Cache of all artifacts in one click. This is useful when you believe there are poisoned artifacts in your cache.

In your team's Build and Deployment settings, [visit the Remote Caching section](https://vercel.com/d?to=%2F%5Bteam%5D%2F%7E%2Fsettings%2Fbuild-and-deployment%23remote-caching&title=Clear+your+team%27s+Remote+Cache) and clear the Remote Cache.

[Visit the docs](https://vercel.com/docs/monorepos/remote-caching#clear-the-remote-cache) to learn more.
