There's _a lot_ to unpack here, but today I want to focus on a skill that I've now witnessed hundreds of people use to improve their design systems before my very eyes: `/ds-inspection`.

Early in the creation of our [AI & Design Systems course](https://aianddesign.systems/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=web&utm_campaign=bf2026&utm_content=blog-2), [TJ](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tpitre) said something like **"You don't want to hitch rocket boosters to the back of a car with its check engine on."** In the same spirit, you don't hook AI rocket boosters up to a design system that's rickety, out of sync, and poorly documented.

That's why TJ created [Figma Console MCP](https://docs.figma-console-mcp.southleft.com/), [FigmaLint](https://www.figma.com/community/plugin/1521241390290871981/figmalint), and a slew of other resources. That's also why **we dedicate an entire chapter of our course to the myriad ways to use AI to improve the quality of your design system foundations.** And that's what led me to create `/ds-inspection`.

The workflow I detail in the course and workshops is this:

1.  **Round up all of your design system assets**: codebase, design library, documentation, and other relevant resources
2.  **Get [Figma Console MCP](https://docs.figma-console-mcp.southleft.com/) up and running**
3.  **Run my [`/ds-inspection`](https://github.com/bradfrost/skills/tree/main/skills/design-systems/ds-inspection) skill** - answer a few questions and let 'er rip
4.  **_Get completely humbled_** by the inspection results. Most people (including me!) received a terrible first score, but good news! You also get a detailed, prioritized plan to improve it
5.  **Blast through improvements to your design system**, improving the quality, accessibility, parity, and AI readiness of your critical UI infrastructure

![Before and after inspection reports for the Eddie Design System: the score rose from 70/100 to 83/100, with most stations turning from yellow to green](https://assets.buttondown.email/images/5cf101af-fe46-4199-8a6b-4462b15ca2e6.png?w=960&fit=max)
