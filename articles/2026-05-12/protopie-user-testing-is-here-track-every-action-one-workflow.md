## [3 Task Types to Match Your Research Goals](#3-task-types-to-match-your-research-goals)

ProtoPie User Testing supports 3 task types, each designed for a different research question:

-   **Exact Path**: The participant follows a specific interaction sequence you define. Use this to validate efficiency: _does the user find the right flow, and how quickly?_ Results include a path match score showing how closely they followed your intended sequence.
-   **Reach Final Scene**: The participant succeeds by arriving at a target scene, regardless of route. Best for open-ended tests: _can users reach the outcome, even via an unexpected path?_
-   **Free Explore**: No success criteria. Users move freely through the prototype while ProtoPie maps the paths taken, time spent, and most common first actions, revealing where attention naturally goes.
