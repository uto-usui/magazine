Enterprise teams can now run Vercel Functions in their own AWS account with [Bring Your Own Cloud](https://vercel.com/docs/networking/bring-your-own-cloud) (BYOC). With BYOC, your function code and execution stay inside your security and compliance boundary. Vercel continues to handle deployments, environments, routing, and static assets.

During deployment, you provision functions in your AWS account and Vercel serves everything else. You keep control over function execution with your own IAM permissions, network access, and resource lifecycle policies.

With BYOC, you can:

-   Run Node.js and Python Vercel Functions in your AWS account
    
-   Meet strict compliance, security, and isolation requirements
    
-   Connect privately to backends and services in your VPC
    
-   Use existing AWS commitments
    

BYOC is available on Enterprise plans. [Contact sales](https://vercel.com/contact/sales) or your account team to get started, or [learn more in the documentation](https://vercel.com/docs/networking/bring-your-own-cloud), and read the [complete guide to BYOC](https://vercel.com/kb/guide/byoc).
