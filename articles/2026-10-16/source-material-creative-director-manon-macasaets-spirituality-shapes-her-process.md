Welcome to Source Material, a weekly series where the designers and creatives shaping culture share the references that shaped them. Our next guest is Manon Macasaet ([@mingblingbling](https://www.instagram.com/mingblingbling/)), a designer, artist, and creative director who founded fashion brand [Poison Candy Apple](https://poisoncandyapple.com/).

After the shoot, we caught up with Manon about her creative process:

**What were you making when you realized, “This could be my job”?**

I realized this could be my job when I started my webseries “[STORY OF MY FUCKING LIFE](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLN6iiGiO3pacsjG3S5qC0CgmJOrr2w-Xn).” It’s one of my more notable video projects, a crunk downtown New York Sesame Street that chronicled my life in 2020. It was a super well-rounded video series that helped me develop most of my visual language, creative direction, and team-building abilities. I need to bring it back.

**What’s the first thing you do when you sit down to work?**

It changes all the time. I try to set an intention, and sometimes I pray. I’m quite a spiritual person and need to feel clear-headed when I’m doing creative work.

**What’s a design object you’d take to your grave?**

My “Helter Skelter” book by Mika Ninagawa, it’s like my style bible.

**Whose work makes you jealous?**

[Mika Ninagawa](https://mikaninagawa.com/). She directed the movie “Helter Skelter,” which is like the OG version of “The Substance.” Or honestly, [Spike Jonze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spike_Jonze). I wish I had directed “Jackass” so bad.

**What current design trend are you tired of seeing?**

Minimalism. I’m bored.

**What rule do you break the most?**

This is a tough one, but when I was filming my web show I broke a lot of rules while sneaking into certain places to get certain shots. You gotta do what you gotta do.

**What song do you put on to get out of a creative rut?**

I’ve been listening to a lot of Snoopy library jazz to get in the zone lately.

**What’s a piece of feedback you resisted, and later realized was right?**

To rest when I feel burnt out. I used to not think this was okay, but now I know it is essential.

**What’s a part of your process that most people don’t know about?**

I think the spiritual piece. I take it very seriously, more than people might know.

**What’s the last thing you came across that made you want to go make something?**

My dear friend Camille has recently become a huge team player in my brand [Poison Candy Apple’s](https://www.instagram.com/poisoncandyapplenyc/) operations. I’m going to make a piece inspired by her because our friendship inspires me daily. My friends and community are a huge part of my world, and in turn, show up in my work.
