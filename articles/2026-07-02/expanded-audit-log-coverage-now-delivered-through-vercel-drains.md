Audit Logs now capture 400+ unique team [activity events](https://vercel.com/docs/activity-log#events-logged), giving teams broader coverage for security reviews, compliance workflows, and investigations.

With Vercel Drains support, teams can export those events to custom HTTP endpoints or Amazon S3, replacing [Custom SIEM Log Streaming](https://vercel.com/docs/audit-log#custom-siem-log-streaming) with a flexible Drains-based workflow.

Audit Log Drains are available for all Enterprise teams, replacing Custom SIEM Log Streaming. They are priced at $0.50/GB through standard Drains pricing, replacing a separate paid add-on.

[Try it out](https://vercel.com/d?to=%2F%5Bteam%5D%2F%7E%2Fsettings%2Fdrains&title=Go+to+Drains+settings) or learn more about [Audit Log Drains](https://vercel.com/docs/drains/reference/audit-logs) and [draining Audit Logs to S3](https://vercel.com/docs/drains/audit-logs-to-s3).
