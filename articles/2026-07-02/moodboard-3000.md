Moodboard 3000 is an image layout engine for Figma. Select images on canvas, choose a layout, and generate a composed moodboard.

Four layout modes: **Grid** for clean uniform layouts, **Editorial** for tight puzzle-like compositions that fill the frame, **Masonry** for Pinterest-style columns, and **Cluster** for organic center-weighted compositions with irregular edges.

Bring your own frame to generate inside any selected frame. Subject-aware crops keep faces and focal points in view. Remix any generated frame for new compositions in the same layout family. Fit to Size re-flows contents when you resize the frame.

Supports images, videos, and GIFs. Adjust gap, margin, image frame styling, corner rounding, and page size. Works with up to 40 images.
