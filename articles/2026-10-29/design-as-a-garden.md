## Design as a garden

A different way to think about creative work.

Mon, 26 Oct 2026

> Don’t demand that things happen as you wish, but wish that they happen as they do happen, and you will go on well.

— Epictetus

1.  _Cultivate the right conditions_. You can’t force a plant to grow at 2pm. But you can tend the soil to give something good the best chance to take root.
2.  _Let different things cross-pollinate_. Interesting work comes from unexpected combinations. Go watch a movie. Read a book outside your field. Talk with someone who sees the world differently.
3.  _Protect what’s fragile_. A young but promising idea can easily be killed before it’s had a chance to develop. Shelter it from premature judgment.
4.  _Come back tomorrow_. Persistence isn’t doing the same thing over and over. Stay with something long enough and you may find another way in.
5.  _Prune what’s not essential_. Space, attention, and resources are limited. Sometimes you have to cut something good to let what is most vital flourish.
6.  _Not every plant needs to bear fruit_. Some plants are worth growing just because you like having them in the garden. Not everything needs a clear payoff.
7.  _Plan for unplanned space_. Don’t fill every inch. Build in room for play, improvisation, and plants you never meant to grow.
8.  _Avoid harvesting too early_. Let an idea fully mature before you ask it to perform. Don’t hurry to pick a direction. Stay open to where else the idea may lead.
9.  _Leave some ground fallow_. Don’t always demand visible results. Step away from time to time. Notice. Absorb. Form new connections. Live a little. It may be quiet on the surface. But below, the soil is renewing itself.
10.  _Match effort to the season_. Ask yourself: does this need more effort — or simply more time? Practice patience. And when the time is ripe, work intensely.
