The **overall UX** performance of the E-cigarette & Vaping sites is “mediocre,” with 5 sites in the “mediocre” and 2 in the “poor” range.

Here are some of the **overarching UX issues**.

The benchmarked E-Cigarette & Vaping sites highlight significant **user experience challenges** across the industry, particularly regarding [product discoverability](https://baymard.com/blog/findability-vs-discoverability-ux), transparency, and data consistency.

While category navigation is plentiful, homepages frequently prioritize [promotional content](https://baymard.com/guidelines/240-ad-looking-content-on-the-homepage) over direct product access, and on-site search systems **often struggle** with [synonyms](https://baymard.com/guidelines/2718-handling-synonyms), [misspellings](https://baymard.com/guidelines/355-handling-misspellings-on-search-results-pages).

Additionally, [filters](https://baymard.com/blog/ecommerce-filter-ui) **omit** essential purchase criteria.

[Critical filters](https://baymard.com/research-articles/5-essential-filters) like price, ratings, and brand are **often absent**, while industry-specific options — such as nicotine strength and blend ratio — [lack explanations](https://baymard.com/research-articles/explain-industry-specific-filters) and [number of product matches](https://baymard.com/guidelines/467-indicating-the-number-of-matches-for-filtering-options).

Furthermore, users face **difficulties comparing products** due to incomplete or inconsistent [list item information](https://baymard.com/research-articles/list-item-design-ecommerce) and [comparison features](https://baymard.com/guidelines/495-comparison-features-in-listings-for-spec-heavy-products), while product pages and carts often fail to provide crucial transparency regarding total order costs (see guidelines [#825](https://baymard.com/guidelines/825-providing-a-total-order-cost) and [#2320](https://baymard.com/guidelines/2320-free-shipping-information-in-the-cart)) and delivery timelines (see [#802](https://baymard.com/guidelines/802-where-and-how-to-display-delivery-information) and [#543](https://baymard.com/guidelines/543-delivery-date-vs-delivery-speed)).

These friction points, combined with fragmented product information [across variations](https://baymard.com/guidelines/848-displaying-core-content-across-product-variations) and opaque, poorly labeled customer account management (see [#2937](https://baymard.com/guidelines/2937-visibility-of-the-account-menu) and [#876](https://baymard.com/guidelines/876-what-to-include-in-the-account-menu)), create a **challenging shopping environment** that hinders efficient product selection and checkout.

In summary, with **no sites** delivering even a “decent” or better overall user experience, there is plenty of room for improvement.
