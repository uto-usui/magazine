## Articles

[Building a general-purpose accessibility agent](https://github.blog/ai-and-ml/github-copilot/building-a-general-purpose-accessibility-agent-and-what-we-learned-in-the-process/) (15min) very interesting insights from Eric Bailey. They used a sub-agent structure that brings the accessibility findings to the main orchestration one. Smart! They also split into reviewer (audit) and implementer (fix) templates. They still end up with ~36% of level A and AA Success Criterion that can’t be automatically discovered, so they still need manual periodic reviews.

[How To Make Your Design System AI-Ready](https://www.smashingmagazine.com/2026/06/how-make-design-system-ai-ready/) (7min) AI cannot magically resolve technical debt or design debt without proper guidance. To help: document design decisions for the AI too, use a linter to keep your Figma files clean and bring the design system specs into a structured markdown file. By Vitaly Friedman.

[A11y 101 – 3.3.4 Error Prevention](https://tarnoff.info/2026/08/03/a11y-101-3-3-4-error-prevention/) (10min) an interesting detailed explanation by Nat Tarnoff  of the 3.3.4 error prevention criteria. While 3.3.3 is about helping fix errors, 3.3.4 is about preventing users from submitting incorrect important information. Think about online purchase, legal information, etc. You must offer a way to confirm, correct and reverse the information before submission. For example: an order summary before a purchase. Or a way to cancel a big transaction within 60 minutes. This is about customer protection!

[You Got Faster. Your Company Didn’t.](https://terriblesoftware.org/2026/06/17/you-got-faster-your-company-didnt/) “I would have written a shorter letter, but I did not have the time.” The Blaise Pascal quote nails it. Matheus Lima explains that the time “saved” by a developer who used AI to make a decision and write code isn’t saved at all. It was just transferred to the next person, who now has even more review time. One person’s shortcut becomes everyone else’s problem. Every individual is now faster, but the whole team isn’t.  He has one rule to help: “if you can’t defend a sentence with the document done, it’s not really done, is it?”
