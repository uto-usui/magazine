## [Copy link to heading](#rogo-on-vercel)Rogo on Vercel

-   73,000+ deployments in a single month
    
-   Agent-written code shipped to production in 5 minutes
    
-   6 production AI agents automating work from churn analysis to deal desk
    
-   Zero manual triage during production incidents with agent swarms on [AI SDK](https://vercel.com/ai-sdk)
    

[Rogo](https://rogo.ai/) builds Felix, an AI agent that generates decks, financial models, and reports for a majority of the world's largest financial institutions. The Rogo team is rearchitecting its internal applications on Vercel, where agent-written code ships in 5 minutes. When incidents hit production, swarms of agents on the [AI SDK](https://vercel.com/ai-sdk) triage and remediate them without manual intervention. As a result, the team now pushes more than 73,000 deployments a month.
