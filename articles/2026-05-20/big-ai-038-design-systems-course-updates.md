[Our course](https://aianddesign.systems/?utm_source=brad_frost&utm_medium=email) now has over **16 hours of videos**, which includes course lessons, community jam sessions, and other demonstrations. TJ, Ian, and I have been busting our asses, and are SUPER pleased with how these new chapters turned out!

-   **Chapter 1 – Core Concepts:** This is a huge chapter that covers the broad arc of technology, the history, value, and challenges of both design systems and AI, how AI & Design Systems together are a powerful combo, and a big orientation into DS+AI tooling & terminology.

And I recorded 20 (!) videos about using Git & GitHub to operate in this modern landscape. I’m really proud that we presented all of this in a way that’s equally useful for beginners and experts alike.
