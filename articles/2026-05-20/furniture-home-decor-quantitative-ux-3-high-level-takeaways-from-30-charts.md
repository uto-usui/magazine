## Confidence in Size and Fit Is a Core Challenge

![Horizontal bar chart showing what product page content helps furniture and home decor shoppers feel confident an item will fit in their space.](https://baymard-assets-cdn.imgix.net/research/media_files/attachments/173066/original/research-media-file-59eab11577c20f3e133921fe6ba98b7d.png?auto=format&dpr=2&fit=max&q=50&w=880)

Written dimensions (49%) and dimensions images (42%) are among the most essential product page content types for helping Furniture & Home Decor shoppers determine whether an item will fit in their space before purchasing online (see [Quantitative Insight #HF037](https://baymard.com/premium/quant-insights/HF037) for an interactive version of this graph alongside key findings and actionable insights).

Furniture and home decor shoppers are often trying to answer a high-stakes question that is **difficult to correct after purchase**: whether a product will fit within their space.

This means furniture and decor shoppers must go **beyond aesthetics and features** to also understand a product’s scale and spatial fit within their home, often using just the product page content.

According to our survey of online furniture and home decor shoppers, the most **essential content types for fit confidence** are written dimensions (49%) and [“Dimension” images](https://baymard.com/blog/dimensions-measurements-product-size-image) (42%).

But our survey also shows that dimensions aren’t enough, as users rely on not just concrete measurements, but also on **visual context** that helps them understand how an item fits within real spaces.

Thus, Furniture & Home Decor product pages should support both measurement-based and **context-based evaluation**, offering content like [reviewer photos](https://baymard.com/blog/allow-reviewers-to-upload-images) (38%), [“In Scale”](https://baymard.com/guidelines/741-visually-indicating-product-scale) images, and [“Lifestyle”](https://baymard.com/guidelines/2461-lifestyle-image-for-product-variations) images (38%) that help users judge scale more intuitively.

## Delivery Details Are a Primary Part of the Purchase Decision

![Horizontal bar chart showing which delivery factors matter most to furniture and home decor shoppers.](https://baymard-assets-cdn.imgix.net/research/media_files/attachments/173065/original/research-media-file-cce830737ee323fa8850356e40f29238.png?auto=format&dpr=2&fit=max&q=50&w=880)

Delivery costs, transparency, and logistics-related details are among the most important factors for Furniture & Home Decor shoppers when ordering online (see [Quantitative Insight #HF047](https://baymard.com/premium/quant-insights/HF047) for an interactive version of this graph alongside key findings and actionable insights).

Delivery is **not just a logistical concern**, but a deciding factor for furniture and home decor shoppers as to whether a purchase is viable in the first place.

Because these products are often heavy, bulky, or delicate, shoppers must factor in delivery logistics, timing, and cost much **earlier in the purchase process** than they might in other categories.

This can include considerations like **fulfillment service levels** (e.g., threshold vs. white-glove), [delivery timing](https://baymard.com/guidelines/2747-delivery-timing-filters-for-relevant-furniture-and-home-decor-products), and [return or cancellation policies](https://baymard.com/guidelines/2757-return-and-cancellation-policies-for-furniture-and-home-decor-products).

Thus, Furniture & Home Decor sites should ensure that key delivery details are clearly communicated early in the journey, including [total order cost](https://baymard.com/guidelines/825-providing-a-total-order-cost), so users **know the full price** before committing.

Sites should also provide [estimated delivery dates](https://baymard.com/guidelines/802-where-and-how-to-display-delivery-information) near the “Buy” section, and offer **[visibility into delivery logistics](https://baymard.com/guidelines/2865-providing-delivery-timing-and-logistics-information)** for large item deliveries.

## Reviews Are Critical for Evaluating Long-Term Product Performance

![Horizontal bar chart showing what information furniture and home decor shoppers look for in reviews.](https://baymard-assets-cdn.imgix.net/research/media_files/attachments/173064/original/research-media-file-fc192415cd1cefd6dafd18d74bf20b0a.png?auto=format&dpr=2&fit=max&q=50&w=880)

Furniture and home decor shoppers use customer reviews as a key source of evidence about a product’s durability, comfort, quality, and ultimately whether it lived up to expectations (see [Quantitative Insight #HF040](https://baymard.com/premium/quant-insights/HF040) for an interactive version of this graph alongside key findings and actionable insights).

Most Furniture & Home Decor shoppers [say they use reviews](https://baymard.com/premium/quant-insights/HF040) to **evaluate long-term quality** and durability when shopping for these products online.

That behavior makes sense in an industry where purchases are often relatively expensive, **expected to last for years**, and [difficult to return](https://baymard.com/premium/quant-insights/HF050I) once delivered and assembled.

In that context, reviews become a **key source of evidence** about durability, comfort (see [#HF041](https://baymard.com/premium/quant-insights/HF041)), and quality, aspects that are only revealed through use over time and can’t be easily assessed from site-provided product page content.

With that, Furniture & Home Decor sites should ensure that reviews are easy to find and scan, and help users to **locate relevant feedback** to their specific concerns by offering [search within reviews or keyword filtering](https://baymard.com/guidelines/742-keyword-filters-and-search-within-reviews).

Sites can also help users quickly evaluate key attributes like **comfort and durability** by surfacing a small number of [contextual review subscores](https://baymard.com/guidelines/767-contextual-review-subscores) when relevant.
