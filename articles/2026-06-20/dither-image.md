## What is Dither Image?

Dither Image is a computer graphics technique used to create the illusion of greater color depth in images that are restricted to a limited color palette. This is achieved by approximating unavailable colors through a diffusion of available colored pixels, which the human eye perceives as a mixed color. Often recognizable by a characteristic graininess or speckled appearance, Dithering is frequently used to convert grayscale images to black and white by varying the density of black dots. Algorithms like the Halftone Dithering Matrix (a clustered method) and the Bayer Matrix (which produces a distinctive cross-hatch pattern) determine how these pixels are arranged.
