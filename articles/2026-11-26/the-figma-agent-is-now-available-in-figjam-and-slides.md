[The Figma agent](https://www.figma.com/blog/6-easy-ways-to-use-the-figma-agent-right-now/)

can help you push an idea further or automate the tasks that slow you down. With connectors and attachments in both products, you can bring in material you’re already working with, like design files, transcripts, or research notes, and ask the agent to organize it on a FigJam board or turn it into a draft presentation.

Here’s how you can get the most out of the agent in FigJam and Figma Slides.

###### You can also use the agent in FigJam to:

→Set up a brainstorming board

→Create a journey map from research and data

→Group sticky notes into themes

→Organize action items in a table with owners and due dates

FigJam is where teams brainstorm, plan, and work together. The agent can help with the preparation and follow-through, giving your team more time for the discussion itself.

### [Gather the right information for your discussion](#gather-the-right-information-for-your-discussion)

FigJam gives your team a shared space to make sense of information scattered across tools. Instead of digging through separate surfaces for the right context, the agent in FigJam brings that context onto the canvas, where your team can compare feedback and spot recurring themes.

[To add a connector](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/35440096186007-Use-verified-partner-MCP-connectors-with-the-Figma-agent-and-Figma-Make) like Notion or Google Drive to a prompt, click `Add context` → `Connectors`, choose the app, and follow the connection prompts.

[To add an attachment](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/31304529835671-Attach-files-to-a-prompt-in-the-Figma-agent-and-Figma-Make) like a screenshot, click `Add context` → `Add images and files` and select your file.

For example, ahead of a design crit, Figma Design Manager Megan Bednarczyk uses the agent to gather the background information she needs to prepare. “There’s usually a lot of context to catch up on,” she says. “What did we hear in research? What came up in Slack? What did we agree on last time?”

Megan starts with the designs already arranged on the board. She uses the agent and connectors to bring in feedback from previous crits and competitor examples alongside them. The agent summarizes previous feedback, the direction the team agreed on, and questions for the upcoming crit. It also adds notes highlighting patterns in the competitor examples.

After the crit, Megan can select the team’s feedback stickies and choose `Organize` → `Summarize`. The agent creates a spec summarizing the discussion, giving the team a starting point for reviewing decisions, open questions, and next steps.

### [Move from debate to decision](#move-from-debate-to-decision)

After a team discussion in FigJam, you may have a board full of feedback to work through. The agent can pull those sticky notes and comments together [into a Spec](https://www.figma.com/blog/introducing-figma-specs/)—a collaborative document that lives on the canvas. You can then review and edit the draft to guide what happens next.

Johnny Dallas, the engineering lead on Specs, uses the agent in FigJam to turn feedback from engineering crits into a draft spec he can review and edit before moving into implementation. “I’ll have a FigJam board that has dozens or hundreds of stickies and comments and opinions from all these smart engineers,” he says. “My job is to take that and actually turn it into code.” But sometimes, it can be hard to come back to a FigJam board after a thoughtful discussion and determine next steps. “In the past when I’ve done crits, I have to go through and read every comment. I might miss some of the quotes, or spend too much time sweating the details. There’s a lot of cleanup,” Johnny explains.

Now, he can select the relevant sticky notes, click `Ask agent`, and prompt his way to a v1, rather than starting from scratch: `Summarize this discussion as a spec. Include decisions, next steps, and open questions, and review the comments on the board, too.` And, because the spec sits alongside the original feedback on the board, he can look back at what the team decided and how they got there.

## [Figma Slides: More room to center the story](#figma-slides-more-room-to-center-the-story)

###### You can also use the agent in Figma Slides to:

→Adapt a deck for a different audience

→Shorten copy across the presentation

→Ask for feedback on your story and slides

→Estimate your presentation’s runtime from speaker notes

When you’re preparing a presentation, you need to decide what to include, how to order it, and what you want the audience to take away. The Figma agent in Figma Slides can help you with starting points and finishing touches, giving you more time to shape the narrative, communicate clearly, and prepare for the audience.

### [Start with the material you already have](#start-with-the-material-you-already-have)

In Figma Slides, you can bring a Spec, attach supporting material, or connect relevant context, and the agent can turn it into a first pass. Jarrod Barretto, a brand designer at Figma, uses the agent this way when working on keynote presentations for Config and internal events. He starts with a script that executives have developed together, then uses the Figma agent to turn that script into slides by prompting: `Break this up into individual slides so the content flows logically.` The agent creates an outline he can review and edit before clicking `Generate deck` to create the slides. “That gives me an architecture—a starting point I can start to fill in,” he says.

### [Spend less time on manual work](#spend-less-time-on-manual-work)

Once you have a first draft of a presentation in Figma Slides, you can ask the agent to take over work like building charts. For example, Jarrod shares a screenshot of a spreadsheet with the agent and prompts it to populate a bar chart from that data. Previously, he’d have to manually draw a chart to scale from scratch. Now, the agent takes on that work. “I can be really precise about how I want a chart to look while letting the agent take care of the mathematical accuracy,” he says. You can also ask the agent to create a chart from scratch using data you provide.

The Figma agent is now generally available in FigJam and Figma Slides on all paid plans, with select connectors and attachments supported in both. Learn more about the [agent in FigJam](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/42091138637079-Work-with-the-Figma-agent-in-FigJam-boards) and [Figma Slides](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/42091139542679-Work-with-the-Figma-agent-in-slide-decks).
