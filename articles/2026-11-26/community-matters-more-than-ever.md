**Sure, we nerd out about design and technology. We sweat tiny details, we argue over semantics, we have strong opinions strongly held about myriad tools & tactics.**

But what's it all for? What is technology for? What is design for? What are systems for? What are websites for? What is our digital world for?

**The answer is people.** The things we create and put out into the world _must_ help improve people's lives. Help people solve problems. Help reduce frustration. Help people connect with one another and the world around them. Help people lead healthier, happier lives. Help people flourish.

Unfortunately, a predatory social media landscape and a loneliness epidemic already existed long before AI hit the scene, but it's clear AI risks exacerbating those issues and pulling us further away from one another. Which of course is antithetical to the mission of helping people lead happier, healthier lives.

**That's why I feel so strongly that community matters more than ever.** It's clear that the pace of change and the vastness of the landscape are increasingly far too much for any one person to keep up with. We need a safe and healthy community to learn, grow, and navigate this moment together.

We've been working hard to create a safe, healthy, and vibrant place away from the hype-y bullshit that feels omnipresent elsewhere. Which is why I LOVE getting feedback like this:

> This community is the least toxic AI space I've encountered. It is so refreshing in that way; everyone seems so helpful and respectful.

_This_ is why we're doing what we're doing. Yes, joining our courses will teach you many practical concepts and tactics, but **the most important part of this whole operation is creating a healthy place (a Slack now 1,000+ strong!) where people can come together to navigate this chaotic landscape, get support, swap notes, commiserate, network, and have fun.** Erin sums it up nicely:

> The courses gave me the big picture, and this community made it feel a lot less lonely to dive in. — _Erin Tozour, Product Designer_

Every other week, our AI & Design Systems course gets together for a community jam session. Everyone asks smart questions, we show off what we're building, and we discuss what's new, exciting, confusing, and emerging in this fast-moving landscape.

We're often joined by wonderful humans/experts/friends like [David Eisner](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dseisner/), the VP of Design at Datadog and author of the hot-off-the-presses book [**Product Design Engineering**](https://rosenfeldmedia.com/books/product-design-engineering/) from the wonderful [Rosenfeld Media](https://rosenfeldmedia.com/).
