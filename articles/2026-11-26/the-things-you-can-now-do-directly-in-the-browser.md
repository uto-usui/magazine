[Agents Didn’t Clear Your Plate. They Dumped Reviews on It](https://www.meticulous.ai/?utm_source=frontend_focus&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=26q4_2&utm_content=primary "www.meticulous.ai") — Your agents write PRs in minutes, then eat your afternoon in review. Meticulous

[Nudeps: npm Imports in the Browser](https://lea.verou.me/blog/2026/nudeps/ "lea.verou.me") — A new tool from Lea Verou and Dmitry Sharabin that lets you `npm install` packages and import them by name directly within the browser, _no bundler required_. It builds upon thinking outlined in Lea's '[Web dependencies are broken](https://lea.verou.me/blog/2026/web-deps/)' article from earlier this year.

Lea Verou

[When Everything Has Changed, What Do We Do Next?](https://css-tricks.com/when-everything-has-changed/ "css-tricks.com") — A post that takes stock of the year we've had, where the AI bubble has undoubtedly reshaped the industry. Andy argues that developers are more than "code slingers," and makes the case for investing in people, building by hand, and keeping independent publishing alive.

Andy Bell

⚡️ IN BRIEF

-   🦊 [Firefox 161 is out](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Mozilla/Firefox/Releases/161) and adds support for the CSS `random()` function (more on that later), plus [Compression Dictionary Transport](https://csswizardry.com/2026/11/web-perf-wednesday-019-firefox-ships-dictionary-compression/#firefox-ships-compression-dictionary-transport).
    
-   🐞 The Ladybird browser (an effort to build a new browser engine _from scratch_) is [now in alpha](https://ladybird.org/posts/alpha/), available for macOS and Linux. You can [download it here](https://ladybird.org/download/).
    
-   ⚙️ Simon Willison has been trying the above Ladybird alpha and [shares his initial thoughts here](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/23/ladybird/), calling it an "_astonishingly ambitious project_".
