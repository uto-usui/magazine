[Speed Insights](https://vercel.com/docs/speed-insights) is deprecating First Input Delay (FID) and will stop recording new measurements on November 1st.

Speed Insights already uses [Interaction to Next Paint (INP)](https://web.dev/blog/inp-cwv-launch), the Core Web Vital that replaced FID, as the responsiveness metric in your [Real Experience Score](https://vercel.com/docs/speed-insights/metrics#real-experience-score-res). The deprecation won’t require changes on your side, affect your score, or prevent you from viewing existing FID data.

Learn more in the [Speed Insights metrics documentation](https://vercel.com/docs/speed-insights/metrics).
