Every Pro team can claim a free domain for one year. That offer now includes `.co`, `.studio`, `.company`, `.info`, `.pro`, `.live`, `.digital`, and `.agency`, bringing the eligible TLDs to sixteen.

The offer still covers one domain per team, and premium domains are not eligible. After the first year, your domain will [renew automatically](https://vercel.com/docs/domains/working-with-domains/renew-a-domain) at the regular price shown at checkout when you claim it.

Claim yours at [vercel.com/domains](https://vercel.com/domains), and see the [free domain FAQ](https://vercel.com/docs/domains/free-domain-with-pro) for the full list of eligible TLDs and terms.
