EditUI

Review your running app in Chrome. Click any element, leave an edit, collect as many as you want, then send them all to Claude Code, Cursor or your coding agent.

① Clicks the heading. Make this heading slightly smaller.

## How it works

Point → Prompt → Collect → Send

1.  01
    
    ### Point
    
    Click anything on your running UI.
    
2.  02
    
    ### Prompt
    
    Describe what you want changed.
    
3.  03
    
    ### Collect
    
    Keep reviewing. EditUI remembers every edit.
    
4.  04
    
    ### Send
    
    Copy the whole review and paste it into your coding agent.
    

## Stop explaining which element you mean.

EditUI captures the context your coding agent needs to understand what you are pointing at.

No screenshots to annotate. No selectors to copy. No source files to hunt down.
