This is a message to managers and decision makers who are mandating their staff to use AI all day with a warning: you’re burning your staff out and setting yourself up for long term problems unless you get back to investing in their skills and satisfying their curiosities.

As always, Sara has put an unbelievably in-depth resource together for us. A must read.

Hiring in tech has slowed to a crawl but there is hope still. Jess has gathered a great list of open opportunities at companies who are not horrible and evil.

They’ve put together a [very useful spreadsheet system too](https://jessica.tech/blog/jobs-spreadsheet/).

A very useful app by Eric to show you what keyboard shortcuts are already claimed by screen readers and apps, for if you’re adding key bindings to what you’re building.

There’s been a lot of people talking about how exciting CSS `random()` is and also plenty mentioning how they’re struggling to understand it. Luckily Mat is very good at explaining complex stuff and he’s brought a nice design pattern as the context with him too.
