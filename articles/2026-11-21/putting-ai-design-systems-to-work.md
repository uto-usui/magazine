## design system ↔ product connection

For as long as atomic design has been around, I've been articulating the connection between design systems and the products they serve. **The design system informs & influences the products, and products inform and influence the design system**.

[![The five stages of atomic design, atoms, molecules, organisms, templates, and pages, each illustrated with pieces of an Instagram-style interface building up to a finished screen](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/fqFGfYXJWQI/maxresdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fqFGfYXJWQI)

### [Atomic Design Creates Connected Design Systems](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fqFGfYXJWQI)

Atomic design connects every layer of a design system to the real products it serves.

This vision is sound, but until recently it's been kinda hand-wavey. But I can now say I'm actually living this vision. I now have a truly system-powered product ecosystem: **changes to Eddie flow into my 20+ products, and each round of product work feeds and strengthens the design system. Hooray, feedback loops!**

Also, on Wednesday **[we introduced skills](https://bradfrost.com/blog/post/skills-to-pay-the-bills/)** and talked about improving your design system foundations. Now we'll highlight some skills I'm developing to improve product quality by building with your design system:

-   **[product-inspection](https://github.com/bradfrost/skills/tree/main/skills/product-design/product-inspection)**: Assess your product's quality using a similar 10-station process as the design system inspection. Get humbled, but get help in improving your product quality
-   **[vibe-killer](https://github.com/bradfrost/skills/tree/main/skills/product-design/vibe-killer)**: (I'm really proud of this name!) converts a vibe-coded page into one built entirely from your design system
-   **[ds-ai-rails](https://github.com/bradfrost/skills/tree/main/skills/design-systems/ds-ai-rails)** - an emerging skill to assess your process for keeping AI on the rails of your design system
-   **[product-to-storybook](https://github.com/bradfrost/skills/tree/main/skills/product-design/product-to-storybook)** sucks your product's screens into your design system's Storybook to make prototyping and front-of-the-front-end work easier
-   **[storybook-to-product](https://github.com/bradfrost/skills/tree/main/skills/product-design/storybook-to-product)** facilitates the process of translating UI you've designed in Storybook into your real product environment

These skills have helped me a ton, but they also need to be put through their paces. **[Give them a try](https://github.com/bradfrost/skills)**, and please don't be shy about filing issues, submitting PRs, and getting in touch!
