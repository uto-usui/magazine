## Design leadership portfolios, annotated.

Leadership Folios collects portfolios from UX Managers, Directors, VPs, Principals, Founders and Executives. Every entry comes with notes on why it works at that level and what you can take from it. New to this? Start with the [Guide](https://www.leadershipfolios.com/guide).

Chosen for what they led, not only what they made. [How portfolios are chosen →](https://www.leadershipfolios.com/about#criteria)
