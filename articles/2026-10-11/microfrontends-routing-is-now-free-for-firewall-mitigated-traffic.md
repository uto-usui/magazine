[Microfrontends Routing](https://vercel.com/docs/microfrontends/routing) is now free for requests that the [Web Application Firewall](https://vercel.com/docs/vercel-firewall/vercel-waf) (WAF) denies, challenges, or rate limits. CDN Requests and Fast Data Transfer are [already free](https://vercel.com/changelog/web-application-firewall-mitigated-traffic-is-free-on-vercel) for this traffic.

The change applies automatically to every project using microfrontends. Learn how to [set up WAF rules](https://vercel.com/docs/vercel-firewall/vercel-waf/custom-rules) in the Firewall documentation.
