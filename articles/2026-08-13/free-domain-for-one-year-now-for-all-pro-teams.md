We recently announced that new Pro teams are eligible to claim [a free domain for their first year](https://vercel.com/changelog/free-domain-now-included-with-new-pro-subscriptions). We are now extending this offer to all [Pro](https://vercel.com/docs/plans/pro-plan) teams.

Existing Pro teams can now also claim a free domain for one year. The offer covers one eligible domain per team across six TLDs: `.online`, `.site`, `.space`, `.store`, `.tech`, and `.website`. After the first year, your domain will [renew automatically](https://vercel.com/docs/domains/working-with-domains/renew-a-domain) at the regular price shown at checkout when you claim it.

Claim yours at [vercel.com/domains](https://vercel.com/domains), and see the [free domain FAQ](https://vercel.com/docs/domains/free-domain-with-pro) for full terms.
