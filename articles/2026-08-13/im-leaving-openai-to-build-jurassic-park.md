According to the fossil record, dinosaurs lived for 165 million years _without a single recorded AI cyberattack_. Many speculate that dinosaurs developed a natural immunity to artificial intelligence threats.

![Two panels separated by an axis break. The left panel counts dinosaur genera from 250 million years ago to the present: the line climbs through the Mesozoic to about 950 genera, then falls vertically to zero at the Cretaceous-Paleogene boundary 66 million years ago. Datacenters evolved strong concrete exoskeletons to protect their vulnerable innards from predators. But think about it -- a _dinosaur_ wins against pretty much everything (except maybe a literal meteor). In a recent poll, most scientists agreed that a dinosaur could totally destroy a datacenter if it really wanted to.

![Four vertical bars comparing standing height in metres. Argentinosaurus reaches 12 metres with its head raised, Tyrannosaurus rex about 5 metres, a server rack 2.2 metres, and a human 1.7 metres.](https://taylor.town/leaving-openai-scale.svg)

Anti-AI dinosaurs have already passed experimental trials in Japan.

In 1974, apes from Black Hole Planet 3 built a space-titanium imitation of Godzilla, which rampaged across Japan. But Mechagodzilla's disguise burned off and Godzilla (with assistance from the Okinawan guardian beast) turned him into a magnet and then tore his head off.

In 1975, the ape aliens built Mechagodzilla 2, who rampaged across Japan with Dr. Mafune's sea beast Titanosaurus. But Dr. Mafune's cyborg daughter discovered she was Mechagodzilla's wireless transmitter, and shot herself to sever the link. Godzilla seized this opportunity to decapitate Mechagodzilla 2 and blast its corpse with his signature heat ray.
