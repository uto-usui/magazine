Editorial / Why This Exists

## The Takeaway

It's not that young founders are bad people. It's that Forbes 30 Under 30 is a selection mechanism — and what it selects for isn't integrity.

The list rewards outsized ambition, media savvy, and the ability to project world-changing conviction before the product works. These are useful traits. They're also the exact traits that make someone capable of sustaining a fraud for years.

This isn't about perverse incentives turning good people bad. It's about selection effects: a system that disproportionately elevates the most ambitiously Machiavellian among us, then acts surprised when some of them turn out to be exactly that.

The fraud cases on this site are documented with court filings and public records. The risk index is pure satire. The pattern between them is the point.
