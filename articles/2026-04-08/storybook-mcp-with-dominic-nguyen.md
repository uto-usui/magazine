[Dominic Nguyen](https://www.linkedin.com/in/domyen/) (Founder of [Storybook](https://storybook.js.org/) and [Chromatic](https://www.chromatic.com/)) joins me to talk about [Storybook MCP](https://storybook.js.org/ai) the long journey of design system quality.

We get into what it actually feels like to be “scaredcited” right now, and Dom shows off the newly-released Storybook MCP. He shows me a demo where AI generates a new search feature using sturdy existing design system components, run it through Storybook’s testing gauntlet, and catch a real accessibility issue in the process.

It was really awesome to see years and years of hard-earned knowledge & features come together to serve this new era. The freaking power of open source software! Just incredible.

If you’re want to learn how to _actually wield_ Storybook MCP and the plethora of other tools out there to help you:

-   make better design systems
-   make better products using AI & design systems
-   invent the future of user experience
