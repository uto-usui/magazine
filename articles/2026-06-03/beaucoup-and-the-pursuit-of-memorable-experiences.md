Beaucoup is a global creative & technology studio founded in 2021 and based in Lyon, France, working internationally across branding, immersive web experiences, and 3D design.

Our vision and approach balance consistency with each brand’s core identity and a constant drive to create memorable experiences.

We approach every project with the same passion and attention to detail.

## Our Journey

Beaucoup was founded by a complementary duo: Lucas, Creative Developer, and Edwin, Art Director. Since then, the team has grown from two founders into a close-knit studio of five people.

The original idea behind the foundation of Beaucoup was simple: a shared passion for crafting thoughtful, well-finished experiences. Today, that same passion resonates throughout the entire team.

Starting from a local network, we quickly expanded beyond borders and began collaborating internationally.

## Orage

ORAGE is a creative post-production studio based in Paris. Combining technical rigor with artistic creativity, they specialize in VFX, motion design, and captivating visuals.

Our goal was to bring that same energy online through a high-tech, immersive interface inspired by computer terminals and film production equipment, where every motion and transition reinforces their creative identity.

**Achievements**

-   Awwwards Site of the Day
-   Awwwards Nominee Site of the Month

## Pebble

Pebble is a California-based startup launching a new generation of high-end electric caravans.

Under the creative direction of [Oui Will](https://www.ouiwill.com/) & [Ashfall](https://ashfall.studio/), we developed and set up the Stripe-powered pre-order experience.

We developed a complex motion-driven journey that both showcases and sells the product, offering an immersive experience alongside a clear and user-friendly pre-order module.

**Achievements**

-   Awwwards Site of the Day
-   Awwwards Nominee Site of the Month
-   Awwwards Nominee E-commerce of the Year

## Mooders

Mooders is a creative music team and sound design studio. Working with images, emotions, and messages, they use sound and music as raw materials to enrich and elevate storytelling.

We helped them redefine both their branding and website, creating a new creative space that allows them to explore their full potential.

The visual direction plays with a black-and-white aesthetic inspired by musical notation, using rhythm in motion as an extension of sound itself.

**Achievements**

-   Awwwards Site of the Day

## Anima

Anima is a biomimicry venture studio dedicated to nature-inspired innovation.

Their multidisciplinary team combines expertise in biomimicry, AI, design, business, and finance to help founders bring ventures from 0 to 1.

We partnered with their team to create a unique 3D universe inspired by organic life, blending natural forms with laboratory aesthetics and AI experimentation.

We then designed an immersive website that highlights this artistic direction while preserving the same atmosphere and vision.

**Achievements**

-   Awwwards Honorable Mention

## Oakame

Oakame is a company based in the north of France, creating unique, elegant, and sustainable furniture made in France.

Their indoor and outdoor furniture is crafted from high-quality oak beams reclaimed from 19th- and 20th-century buildings.

We partnered with their team to create a unique e-commerce experience inspired by raw materials and craftsmanship, built around an editorial layout.

The interface was designed to remain as clear and intuitive as possible, encouraging users to explore, customize, and purchase products seamlessly.

**Achievements**

-   Awwwards Site of the Day
-   Awwwards Nominee Ecommerce of the Year

## Caeli Energie

Caeli is reinventing air conditioning through a revolutionary, energy-efficient system designed to respect both people and the planet.

We partnered with Caeli Energie to bring their bold vision to life, from branding and web design to 3D visuals and creative direction.

We created a fresh, minimal identity that reflects their clean technology and innovative spirit, supported by immersive 3D visuals and a website designed for clarity and impact.

**Achievements**

-   Awwwards Site of the Day
