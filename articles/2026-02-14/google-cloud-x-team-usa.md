## Google Cloud  
x Team USA

As a partner of Team USA, Google Cloud collaborated with elite athletes to build an AI tool that transforms standard video into actionable performance data, helping athletes find their competitive edge for the Olympic Winter Games.

![Google Cloud x Team USA - Official Partner](https://sports.withgoogle.com/assets/logos/GC_WhiteBkgrnd_FlagwithRings_OfficialPartner_RGB.svg)

Historically, elite skiers and snowboarders lacked the data needed to safely push their limits.

Google Cloud is helping by providing AI to measure extreme human movement without the use of sensors.

### The physics no one could see

Google Cloud AI helps athletes understand their performance by measuring every phase of a trick.

![Coaching insights with Gemini icon](https://sports.withgoogle.com/assets/tool-bts/icon-coaching-gemini.svg)

### Coaching insights with Gemini

Gemini synthesizes these metrics and explains them in plain language. What happened, why it matters, and what to try next.

![3D pose icon](https://sports.withgoogle.com/assets/tool-bts/icon-3d.svg)

### 3D pose

How the body spins, compresses, and holds its line through the air. Cumulative rotational degrees, rotational velocity, axis angle, tuck compression—captured frame by frame.

![Flight dynamics icon](https://sports.withgoogle.com/assets/tool-bts/icon-flight.svg)

### Flight dynamics

The path through air and space. To visualize movement, the team developed new ways to render the body's trajectory—capturing amplitude, speed, and the shape of the cork.

To ensure athletes and viewers can truly "read" a run, we developed custom graphics that reveal the physics behind the sport.

The Cork Ribbon maps the chaotic axis of a spin into a clear, trackable trajectory, visualizing the shape of the cork in mid-air.

![Performance analysis visualization](https://sports.withgoogle.com/assets/broadcast/bts-pic-gsv3.jpg)

![Performance analysis visualization](https://sports.withgoogle.com/assets/broadcast/bts-pic-012.jpg)

Paired with skeletal tracking, which captures body mechanics frame-by-frame, these tools break down the nuance of every trick—turning a split-second blur into a precise story of performance.
