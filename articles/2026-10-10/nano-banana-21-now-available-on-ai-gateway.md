[Nano Banana 2.1](https://vercel.com/ai-gateway/models/gemini-nano-banana-2.1) from Google is now available on [AI Gateway](https://vercel.com/ai-gateway) as `google/gemini-nano-banana-2.1`.

Compared with earlier Nano Banana models, it improves image generation and editing, including product recontextualization, mask- and ink-based editing, and factuality. In practice, that means placing products in new scenes, limiting edits to masked or marked regions, and depicting real-world subjects more accurately.

It also renders photorealistic skin tones, detailed materials, sharp lighting, and coherent backgrounds while maintaining Flash-tier latency and cost.

Set `AI_GATEWAY_API_KEY`, then use the model through the [AI SDK](https://vercel.com/docs/ai-gateway/modalities/image-generation/ai-sdk), the OpenAI-compatible [Chat Completions API](https://vercel.com/docs/ai-gateway/sdks-and-apis/openai-chat-completions/image-generation), or [AI CLI](https://github.com/vercel-labs/ai-cli):

Try Nano Banana 2.1 in [imagen.sh](https://imagen.sh/) or the [model playground](https://vercel.com/ai-gateway/models/gemini-nano-banana-2.1). See the [image generation documentation](https://vercel.com/docs/ai-gateway/modalities/image-generation) for additional configuration and editing examples.

Browse all [image models](https://vercel.com/ai-gateway/models?type=image) available on AI Gateway.
