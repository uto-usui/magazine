Summary:  Unsure where to start? Use this collection of links to our articles and videos to learn about how artificial intelligence works, and how users think about it.

Let’s not pretend we all completely understand the inner workings of artificial intelligence models. Neither do your users. Thankfully, you don’t need to fully understand them to use them. The same has always been true of a computer.

However, the better you understand some of the basics, the more accurate your [mental model](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/mental-models/) is. Accurate mental models make you more efficient and help you avoid problems.

These resources can **strengthen your mental model of how AI functions** and **illuminate the mental models your users have for these systems**, which dictate their usage of them.

## Trust and Anthropomorphism

People inherently seek interactions with people (or systems) that seem to [listen to them and understand their needs](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/autonomy-relatedness-competence/). The better the AI is at seeming as though it understands a user, the more they trust it and project human-like attributes on it.

**Number**

**Link**

**Format**

**Description**

1

[Prioritize Smarts over Sentience to Increase Trust with AI](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/smarts-emotion-trust-ai/)

article

People trust AI more when it seems smart rather than sentient. AI emotions can reduce trust in factual, task-oriented work.

2

[The ELIZA Effect - Why We Love AI](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/eliza-effect-ai/)

article

Users quickly attribute human-like characteristics to artificial systems, which reflect their personality back to them.

3

[ELIZA Effect: Why We Fall in Love With AI](https://www.nngroup.com/videos/eliza-effect/)

video

4

[When Should We Trust AI? Magic-8-Ball Thinking and AI Hallucinations](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ai-magic-8-ball/)

article

It’s easy to place too much trust in genAI tools. Use only information you can verify or recognize to be true.

5

[The 4 Degrees of Anthropomorphism of Generative AI](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/anthropomorphism/)

article

Users tend to anthropomorphize AI at four different levels: courtesy, reinforcement, roleplay, and companionship.

6

[Humanizing AI Is a Trap](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/humanizing-ai/)

article

Large-language models (LLMs) humanize by design, and adding personality or emotion amplifies the risk of misplaced user trust.

LLMs have introduced a new way for users to seek answers to their questions — a role search engines have dominated for years. While AI has made certain aspects of information seeking easier, it is far from perfect. It remains difficult to know whether its answers are true and trustworthy.
