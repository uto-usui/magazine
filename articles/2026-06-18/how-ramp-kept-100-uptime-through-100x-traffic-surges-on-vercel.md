## [Link to heading](#ramp-on-vercel)Ramp on Vercel

-   Full site migration to Next.js on Vercel
    
-   100% uptime and 0% error rates through 100x surges
    
-   AI SDK implemented to build semantic search across scattered internal assets
    

Ramp is a financial operations platform that combines bill payments, corporate cards, procurement, and travel to help businesses save time and money. The team rebuilt its marketing site on Next.js and Vercel, migrating its highest-traffic and highest-conversion pages one at a time and A/B testing each against Core Web Vitals.

The site has held 100% uptime through traffic surges of 100x during stunt-marketing events, and the team is now using the Vercel AI SDK to build semantic search across disparate internal assets.
