-   Time-to-prototype cut by 75%

Code and Theory is a digital-first creative and technology agency that combines strategy, design, and engineering for brands like Microsoft, Amazon, and NBC. The agency replaced wireframes and requirement documents with prompt-to-code workflows in v0, so product, engineering, and go-to-market teams can turn a client brief into a working prototype in real time.
