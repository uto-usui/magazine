New [Pro](https://vercel.com/docs/plans/pro-plan) subscriptions now include a domain free for the first year.

The offer covers six TLDs:

`.online` `.site` `.space` `.store` `.tech` `.website`

Each team has 30 days after subscribing to claim one domain, either at checkout or later from the [domain search](https://vercel.com/domains) in the dashboard.

After the first year, your domain will [renew automatically](https://vercel.com/docs/domains/working-with-domains/renew-a-domain) at the regular price shown at checkout when you claim it.

See the [free domain FAQ](https://vercel.com/docs/domains/free-domain-with-pro) for the full terms.
