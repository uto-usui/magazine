AI companies have spent the past year hiring forward-deployed engineers: engineers who sit inside a customer’s team, hear the problem firsthand, and ship against it. One count of 1,000 listings found the role grew 1,165% in a single year. This year I did a version of that job for my own newsroom, without the title, and it changed how I think about listening.

Being heard runs on two clocks. Listening should be slow. Response should be fast.
