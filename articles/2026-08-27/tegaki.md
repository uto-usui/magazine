Generate

The generator CLI downloads any Google Font, extracts glyph outlines, computes skeletons, and traces stroke paths with width and timing data.

Bundle

Stroke data is packaged into a compact bundle with glyph metrics and timing information.

Render

Drop a component into your app — React, Svelte, Vue, SolidJS, Astro, Web Components, or vanilla JS. It handles text layout, line wrapping, and smooth animation playback.

Stream

Animate handwriting as text streams in from an API — perfect for AI chat interfaces.
