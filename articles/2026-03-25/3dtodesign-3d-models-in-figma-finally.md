## Design with your 3D assets

Load a 3D model, adjust the angle, and capture it directly onto your canvas. It can even be readjusted later on.

## How to import a 3D model into Figma?

1.  In Figma, go to the action menu and search for "3d.to.design" plugin
2.  Load your 3D model (glTF, GLB, or OBJ supported) — it's instantly added to your canvas.
3.  Rotate and pan to get the perfect angle — the canvas updates automatically.

## Supported formats

1.  glTF (.gltf)
2.  GLB (.glb)
3.  OBJ (.obj)

## Easy editing

Re-select any imported layer and the plugin automatically reloads your model with the same view. Adjust the angle and re-import — no need to start over.

## Controls

1.  Drag to rotate the model
2.  Hold ⌘ and drag to pan

## Try it out

Not sure where to start? The plugin comes with sample models you can play with right away — no files needed.
