## 1Design

### Timeless character

Flat terminals, gently squared curves, and a balanced x-height give Paper Mono the familiar character of a classic sans.

Quartz & Grain  
Regal Graphics  
jagged inkwell

### Variable weights

You have 8 weights to work with, and the variable font lets you fine-tune the weight anywhere in between.

Thin 100

Regular 400

Extrabold 800

### Beautiful numbers and symbols

We designed numbers and symbols with utmost care, so that they’re pleasing to look at and effortless to read.

1234567890  
$1,280.50 × 1%  
@ & # ½ x²

### Box-drawing characters

Build tables, diagrams, and progress bars directly in text, using 128 box-drawing characters and 32 blocks.

By default, the zero is unslashed to keep the numerals looking clean. You can add a slash when you need to tell 0 and O apart.
