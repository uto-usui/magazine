## Most popular content this week

[You Don’t Have a Design System](https://mattrothenberg.com/notes/you-dont-have-a-design-system/) (14min) Matt Rothenberg gave AI agents the same component library and ended up with 3 different setting pages. Because component libraries often don’t document the kind of product patterns that you need in order to bring consistency across all the pages. Honestly, you don’t even need to bring AI to the mix: give the same pattern libraries to 3 designers, if you don’t have strong documentation on the expected hierarchy, navigation guidelines, etc, you will end up with each designer building their own thing. His solution is to write those strong rules down. But here, again, I wonder: it’s kind of easy to force AIs to follow the rules, but how do you ensure the designers do it as well? (yeah, I know, governance hehe)

This resonated a lot, because I’m currently building a new design system, in a place where we want not only to standardize the components, but also the UX patterns. If you want to participate in the discussion, you can [reply the my post on Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/stephaniewalter.bsky.social/post/3mtjqxhv6dm2t) or [reply to the post on Mastodon](https://front-end.social/deck/@stephaniewalter/117128980088966343).

## Articles

[AI is removing the middle class of software engineering](https://blog.florianherrengt.com/ai-removing-middle-class-software-engineering.html) (15min) “AI will make the best people much more productive and the bad ones almost impossible to hire. Before, there was a good chance someone would catch their bad decisions before they went too far. Now they can make changes faster than anyone around them can realistically review or understand them.”. A very interesting point of view, from Florian Herrengt. I wonder if this will become true for designers as well?

[Claude Code 101, for Designers](https://yaronschoen.com/blog/claude-code-101-for-designers/) (10min) an introduction to understand what Claude code is, with lots of definitions of its basic concepts. Whether you use AI or not, I think it’s still quite nice to understand what it’s about, and the main concepts around it. By Yaron Schoen

[Jimothy’s Acceptance Should Extend to Disabled People Too](https://gracedowwrites.com/2026/07/23/jimothys-acceptance-should-extend-to-disabled-people-too/) (5min) we have to acknowledge the uncomfortable irony that a disabled animal is more accepted in society than a disabled person. We need to apply this acceptance towards disabled people: accept that a life with disability is a full and valuable life, and worthy of respect as it is.

## Interesting websites

[in Frame](https://allinframestock.com/), a free stock photo collection to help bring better disability representation in marketing, advertisement, design and beyond. It’s led by disabled creative people and contains more than 250 images. The collection captures the full range of everyday life: at work, at home, and everywhere in between. Real people. Real moments. No clinical tropes.
