### **Senior Designer**

We are looking for a senior designer to work on Widgetsmith and our other apps!

#### **Position Details:**

-   Freelance/contract work 
    
-   Estimated Hours: Flexible up to 40 hrs/week
    
-   Rate commensurate with experience
    
-   Fully remote; preferably based in the US or UK
    

#### **Required Abilities:**

-   Be familiar and comfortable with current iOS design trends
    
-   Be proficient with Adobe Creative Suite, Sketch, and related software
    
-   Ability to work in a collaborative environment where priorities may rapidly change as new things come up
    
-   Must have the ability to coordinate multiple tasks and projects within set time periods
    

#### **Essential Tasks:**

-   Developing aesthetic themes for widgets in [Widgetsmith](https://bit.ly/)
    
-   Work on new widget types for Widgetsmith
    
-   Create new wallpapers for Widgetsmith
    
-   Work on future updates for [Pedometer++](https://pedometer.app/) and [Sleep++](https://sleepplusplus.app/)
    
-   Create and maintain App Store promotional assets
    
-   Create imagery and videos for use across social channels
