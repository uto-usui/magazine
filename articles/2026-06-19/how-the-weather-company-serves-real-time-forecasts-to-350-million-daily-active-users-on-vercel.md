## [Link to heading](#the-weather-company-on-vercel)The Weather Company on Vercel

-   350 million monthly active users served worldwide
    
-   2.2 billion coordinates calculated every 15 minutes
    
-   80% increase in velocity from design to live web page
    

The Weather Company delivers weather forecasting and content to over 350 million monthly active users worldwide, backed by about 130 meteorologists tracking weather systems across the globe. By rebuilding its web serving stack and CMS on Vercel and v0, the team turned work that once took years into months and shortened the path from a design to a published website from days to hours.
