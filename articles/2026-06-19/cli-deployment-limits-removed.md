We've removed CLI-specific deployment limits, making it easier to deploy from local machine and external CI/CD pipelines with instant feedback. Teams and AI agents can now deploy at the pace their workflows demand.  

Learn more about limits in the [Documentation](https://vercel.com/docs/limits).
