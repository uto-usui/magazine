## A11YEXPERIENCEGUIDE

An accessibility (a11y) specification for experience designers. Synthesizing complex compliance standards into clear, actionable design rules.

## The Synthesis

This guide distills 15 years of W3C standards (WCAG 2.0, 2.1, & 2.2) into ten actionable experience themes. It translates 60+ technical success criteria into clear, usable interaction rules for modern product teams.

## The Urgency

With tightening digital regulations globally (ADA Title II, European Accessibility Act 2025), accessibility compliance is no longer optional; it is a critical requirement for legal safety and market reach.
