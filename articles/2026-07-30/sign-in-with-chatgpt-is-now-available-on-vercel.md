Sign in with ChatGPT adds your ChatGPT account as an authentication option for Vercel. It is available when adding the Vercel plugin to ChatGPT and when signing in to Vercel or v0.

When you add the Vercel plugin to ChatGPT, you can easily sign in to Vercel and grant team and project permissions without leaving ChatGPT.

"Continue with ChatGPT" also appears as a sign-in option on the Vercel and v0 sign-in and sign-up pages. Team requirements like 2FA and SSO still apply. You can re-authenticate or disconnect the ChatGPT sign-in method in your account [Authentication settings](https://vercel.com/account/settings/authentication).

Sign in with ChatGPT is in beta. [Add the Vercel plugin to ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugin_connector_690a90ec05c881918afb6a55dc9bbaa1) to try it and [read OpenAI's changelog](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/changelog#codex-2026-07-29) to learn more.
