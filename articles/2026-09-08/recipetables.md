## What's a recipe table?

A prep-table notation invented by Michael Chu at Cooking for Engineers: ingredients down the left, each step a merged cell spanning exactly what it combines. The whole recipe reads in one look, with no scrolling back and forth between the ingredient list and the instructions. [The full argument](https://recipetables.com/why), if you want it.

RecipeTables converts any recipe into that format. Paste the text, drop a link, or photograph a cookbook page, then scale the servings, switch between cups and grams, and export the table as an image, spreadsheet, or print-ready page. With a free account, every table you make lands in your recipe box: the standard format your collection never had. Link conversions join [the library](https://recipetables.com/recipes), credited back to their source.
