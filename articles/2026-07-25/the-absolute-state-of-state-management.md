[The Absolute State of Management](https://infrequently.org/2026/07/state-management/ "infrequently.org") — Alex Russell argues Redux, MobX, Zustand, and React itself don't 'manage state', they just propagate it whereas 'real' state management means handling time and conflicts, the way CRDTs and sync engines do. Deliberately provocative (Redux maintainer Mark Erikson has [already pushed back](https://bsky.app/profile/acemarke.dev/post/3mrdi65bsdc2y)) but with substance underneath.
