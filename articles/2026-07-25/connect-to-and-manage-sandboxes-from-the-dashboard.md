You can now connect to any running Sandbox from the Vercel dashboard. Run commands, browse the filesystem, upload and download files, and inspect open ports without leaving the browser.

Vercel Sandbox interactive terminal

From the same view, you can also manage the sandbox lifecycle: take snapshots, and stop or resume persistent sandboxes. Open the [Connect tab](https://vercel.com/d?to=%2F%5Bteam%5D%2F%5Bproject%5D%2Fsandboxes&title=Go+to+Sandboxes) on any sandbox to try it out.

Learn more about Vercel Sandboxes in the [documentation](https://vercel.com/docs/sandbox).
