Yesterday, I sent out a [newsletter](https://bradfrost.com/newsletter/) and wanted to share it here too because HOLY CRAP. It's been nearly two months since I've published a newsletter or substantive blog post, to which I say, “[No sir, I don't like it](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cDGlN6mluGA).”

I'm tired of saying I'm busy (it's unhealthy, not a badge of honor), but it continues to be the truth. Just how busy, you ask? Well, [I had a fantastic conversation with Don Norman](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ryyYeJGEkQQ&list=PLRVyFmnlMfe0lMYaPF6zcT2JVUdpAjg9-&index=5) (one of my all-time heroes whom I owe my career to) and literally haven't talked about it at all. Oof.

I've had to learn the hard way that there's a reason why people seclude themselves in cabins to create big projects. So while I'm still hunkered down with my big projects, my [alarm clock](https://bradfrost.com/blog/post/blog-alarm-clock/) went off so I need to come up for air, say hey, and share some big updates.

## Exciting news: my website looks like crap!

If you visit [bradfrost.com](https://bradfrost.com/), you'll see that it looks like crap. Believe it or not, that's intentional.

![A screenshot of Brad's work-in-progress website that kinda intentionally looks like crap.](https://bradfrost.com/images/ig9o0KuN5Z-960.png)

I even got the old-school construction GIF in there.

My website has been my online home and primary means of expression for my entire adult life. Which is why it's a big deal to say that after \*\*19 YEARS\*\* (🤯) of bradfrost.com running on \[WordPress\](https://wordpress.org/), I just migrated the website to the fantastic \[Eleventy\](https://www.11ty.dev/). (Now's a perfect time to \[subscribe/update via RSS\](https://bradfrost.com/feed/blog.xml)).

With the platform migration done, **I'm now redesigning/rebuilding it using our** [**Eddie design system**](https://ds.bradfrost.com/) **as video lessons for our** [**AI & Design Systems course**](https://aidesignsystems.com/)**.** I'm putting my money where my mouth is: walking through the entire end-to-end process of the most important website in my life (and the lifeblood of my business), teaching how to use design systems & AI together to make a beautiful, effective, and successful website.

## Is that it???

At the beginning of this year, I [declared systems bankruptcy](https://bradfrost.com/blog/post/declaring-systems-bankruptcy/) and I've spent the entire year blowing up, overhauling, building, and bolstering all of the infrastructure and systems in my life and work. It's been difficult-yet-necessary work to do alongside getting our real projects out the door. All of this systems work is starting to bear fruit and will hopefully result in a saner, more sustainable pace moving forward. Holy shit, it better!

With this whirlwind of activity, my body, mind, and soul are looking forward to a much-needed break. Thankfully, next week I get to be a beach bum at the Outer Banks on our long-standing annual family vacation.

I hope your summer has been going well and that you've been doing interesting and restorative things! See ya soon.
