The Cube Rule

[![The Cube Rule of Food for identifying dishes based on starch locations](https://cuberule.com/assets/15_cube_rule.jpg)](https://twitter.com/Phosphatide/status/974067376894328833)

identify any food

purely by the location

of structural starch
