> This is the home of the [Tiny Awards](https://tinyawards.net/), which, since 2023, has celebrated the best of the small, poetic, creative, handmade web.
> 
> The Tiny Awards exist because we thought it was important to shine a spotlight on the sorts of personal web projects that tend to get overlooked by more traditional, larger or more commercial awards.
> 
> Each year we ask the online world to nominate their favourite creative, non-commercial website built in the past 12 months.
