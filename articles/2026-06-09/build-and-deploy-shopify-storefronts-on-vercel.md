You can now create a [Shopify store directly from Vercel and](https://v0.app/?pi=shopify) start building a production-ready storefront in minutes.

[Install the Shopify integration](https://vercel.com/marketplace/shopify) to automatically configure your Shopify credentials in Vercel. Create a free test store, build with [v0](https://v0.app/?pi=shopify) and deploy without leaving your workflow. When you're ready to launch, you can claim the store and take ownership of it.

```
vc i Shopify
```

Coming soon: Connect an existing Shopify store to Vercel.

Get started by installing [Shopify from the Vercel Marketplace](https://vercel.com/marketplace/shopify), [v0](https://v0.app/) , or [Vercel CLI](https://vercel.com/docs/cli/integration) to start building your next [commerce experience](https://vercel.com/templates/next.js/shopify-online-store-with-next-js).
