## Articles

[Wait, who made this?. The rise of creative provenance](https://uxdesign.cc/wait-who-made-this-705a30d74220) (6min) with the rise of AI, people don’t trust anymore that something was made by a human. Some companies surf on this trend and bring more and more “behind the scenes” to prove it’s human made. The process is becoming the product (or part of the product). Still, even those are curated, so you never know. Allie Paschal argues that, whether the provenance matters depends on the type of project. For expressive work (like the Odyssey movie), people would not be happy if it was entirely AI made. But most would be okay or not care, if AI was used to build systems utilities or transactional experience (for example a productivity dashboard). Also, for the fun, I asked an AI if this article was AI generated (because why not?). The answer is something like “there are some sentences that smell AI but a lot of human touch, so, more likely human written but AI polished.

[Make It Work vs. Make It Good](https://blog.jim-nielsen.com/2026/make-it-work-make-it-good/) (4min)  “Make it work” is the first 90% of the work. “Make it good” is the other 90%. Yup, a great analysis by Jim Nielsen

## Inspiration & Illustrations

[KantaKerro](https://kantakerro.com/) is a siberian Artist based in Malaysia with an amazing talent for working light into illustrations.

[Larisa Kravets](https://www.saatchiart.com/loraaart) is an illustrator based in Lisbon. She works a lot with ink and watercolor and explores themes of identity, transformation, mythology, solitude, and the relationship between humanity and nature. I love the black and white style and the way she manages to convey motion in static drawings. Don’t forget to also [check out her instagram](https://www.instagram.com/loraaart).

[Mortale Lethe](https://www.mortalelethe.com/portfolio) is a French illustrator. I love her tarot card style characters inspired by Greek, Roman and Egyptian mythology. Also [check out their instagram](https://www.instagram.com/mortalelethe).

I don’t want to influence your votes but honestly the H proposal for [the new European bank notes looks amazing](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/euro/banknotes/future_banknotes/html/design-proposals.en.html) and you should totally give it a good grade!

## Useful tools & resources

[Sharp](https://sharp.pixelplumbing.com/) is an image processing tool that lets me reduce the size of all images on my blog using the command line. I was using Squoosh but it’s not maintained anymore, so sharp is my new squoosh. Those names, haha.

[Afro Index](https://afroindex.org/) is an ongoing reference library for Black hairstyles. This is super useful for creatives who want to draw more diverse characters. Explore the styles names and filter, and get reference images.

## Frameworks and concepts

[The COM-B Model for Behavior Change](https://thedecisionlab.com/reference-guide/organizational-behavior/the-com-b-model-for-behavior-change) (15min) if you are interested in behavioral science, you need to learn about the COM-B model: any change needs capability, opportunity and motivation. This is often used at higher levels to change human behavior in their daily lives, but we could also imagine using it for adoption and change management for new tools or new processes within an organisation for example.
