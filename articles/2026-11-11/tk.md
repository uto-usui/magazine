## Why “TK”?

In publishing, “TK” means “to come,” a placeholder writers leave when a fact or phrase isn’t ready yet. It’s a promise to return later, so your writing can keep moving.

We built TK around that same principle, but added more power to your placeholders so you can put them to work. Whenever you hit a momentum killer, you can tap in TK’s custom AI assist and let it fill in the gaps without derailing your train of thought.

Type…

@TK the year this law passed

@TK find the sentence I wrote about this last week

@TK hyperlink the blog post I’m talking about

…and AI leaves you to do the thinking only you can do.

## How we think about AI

You do the writing and the thinking. TK’s assistant is there to make both easier.

TK won’t write your essay for you. It gives you a place to write and a way to find your ideas later. We built it for people who want to do more of their own writing.

Here’s where AI does show up:

In the editor. Type @tk to check a fact, dig up something you wrote last month, or get unstuck. Otherwise it stays out of your way.

In feedback. Before you show a draft to anyone, you can ask for a quick read on structure or a second opinion on your headline.

In your archive. It organizes and summarizes your notes so you can find that idea from last spring.

Your writing is yours. It’s private until you choose to share it, and you control who sees it. No AI company trains on your writing or uses it without your permission. Write about a trip to Lisbon and you won’t see hotel ads in your feeds that afternoon.
