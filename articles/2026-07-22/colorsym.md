The font is released under the SIL Open Font License, which means you can create your own variants and submit them to be included in the official collection.

Create your unique style while maintaining the symbol mappings.

#### 3\. Submit a Pull Request

Share your creation with the community for review and inclusion.

## Built by designers who care

ColorSym was born from our shared belief that a great design should include everyone.

![Chris Eastridge](https://colorsym.com/assets/chris-eastridge-Dd_UE4_t.webp)

### Chris Eastridge

Co-Creator

[Moon Saga Workshop](https://www.moonsagaws.com/)

Game designer, software engineer, and founder of Moon Saga Workshop. Chris created the original color symbol system for his tabletop game Parks & Potions.

![Luis Francisco](https://colorsym.com/assets/luis-francisco-C_pV-Om8.webp)

### Luis Francisco

Co-Creator

Board game entrepreneur behind Grok Games and Boardssey. Luis expanded Chris's symbol system into ColorSym, an open standard for color accessibility.

### The Story Behind ColorSym

Color accessibility in board games has always been an afterthought. No universal system existed that was free, flexible, and actually readable from any direction. Chris built the first solution for his own game, _Parks & Potions_. Luis saw something bigger in it. Together, they turned a practical fix into ColorSym: an open standard anyone can use, anywhere color needs to be understood.

A collaboration rooted in accessibility, creativity, and open design
