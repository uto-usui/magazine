[Vercel MCP](https://vercel.com/docs/agent-resources/vercel-mcp) now supports purchasing Vercel products. You can:

-   Upgrade your team to the [Pro plan](https://vercel.com/docs/plans/pro-plan)
    
-   Add prepaid credits for [v0](https://v0.app/docs) (requires a paid v0 plan) or [AI Gateway](https://vercel.com/docs/ai-gateway)
    
-   Purchase the [SIEM add-on](https://vercel.com/docs/audit-log#custom-siem-log-streaming) (requires an Enterprise plan)
    
-   [Purchase and register a domain](https://vercel.com/docs/domains/working-with-domains#buying-a-domain-through-vercel)
    

Vercel MCP quotes the price, explains whether the charge is one-time or recurring, and completes the purchase only after you confirm. When an exact price isn't available, it links to the relevant pricing or billing page. Purchases require a team role with billing access and a valid payment method.
