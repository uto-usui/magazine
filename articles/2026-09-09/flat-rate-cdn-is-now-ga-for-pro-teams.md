[Flat Rate CDN](https://vercel.com/docs/manage-cdn-usage#flat-rate-cdn) is now generally available for Pro teams. Flat Rate CDN is an alternative to usage-based CDN billing that provides you with a fixed monthly bill.

Monthly Flat Rate CDN capacity includes:

-   Fast Data Transfer
    
-   Blob Data Transfer
    
-   CDN Requests
    
-   Observability events generated by CDN requests
    

Capacity is set at the team level, not the project level, so a spike on a single project draws on the team-level allowance.

Spike protection is on by default. With spike protection, traffic above your tier's capacity is served normally, without degraded performance, and isn't billed (subject to the [fair use guidelines](https://vercel.com/docs/limits/fair-use-guidelines)).

1M requests and 1 TB of data transfer are included with Pro. Additional monthly capacity can be purchased by upgrading to a higher Flat Rate CDN tier.
