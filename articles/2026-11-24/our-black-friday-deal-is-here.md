## [Every Black Friday is important for independents like us, but this year it’s especially important](#every-black-friday-is-important-for-independents-like-us-but-this-year-its-especially-important)

It’s been…a year, huh? For independent publishers like us, it’s been nothing short of disastrous, [as I summarised a few weeks ago](https://bell.bz/in-response-to-the-death-of-web-development-education/). I’m being deliberately blunt here because I don’t think people realise _how bad_ things have gotten, [even compared to last year](https://bell.bz/its-been-a-very-hard-year/).

We all know the _why_: generative AI has completely taken over the industry and that might have triggered thoughts like “I don’t need to bother learning now because I’ve got an LLM”. I don’t think that’s a viable, long term viewpoint though.

Look, I _get it_, the technology really can feel like it’s a miracle machine, but it’s important to take a wider view. Along with the [published studies on cognitive decline](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.00181v1) and the [widening skills gap](https://eu.usatoday.com/story/money/2026/02/27/ai-entry-level-work-jobs/88590459007/), it’s more important than ever to invest in **your brain** to keep your skills sharp because you’re not getting that **repetitive reinforcement that writing code every day gives you** anymore.

If you’re using an LLM for work every day — either by choice or by [mandate](https://piccalil.li/blog/are-peoples-bosses-really-making-them-use-ai/) — you should theoretically have a lot more time to invest in your learning and personal development. If you’re a boss reading this, you should be giving your developers more time to learn, rather than chasing endless _volume_. It’s going to avoid burnout which yep, [daily LLM use will likely cause in the long term](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.10050). I [wrote in more detail about where we currently are and how to navigate the future over on CSS-Tricks](https://css-tricks.com/when-everything-has-changed/) last week too, which I encourage bosses to read, especially.

Black Friday really is the most important time of year for educators, so we’d be delighted if you could consider our [three _excellent_ courses](https://piccalil.li/courses/), including [JavaScript for Everyone, which has just had a _huge_ update](https://piccalil.li/links/javascript-for-everyone-new-game-plus/). Know that funds from every course purchase are equally distributed between author and publisher here and everything we, the publisher take, is immediately invested back into providing great education for the community.

At the end of the day, [if we stop, we lose](https://piccalil.li/blog/if-we-stop-we-lose/), so support from the industry and community is essential and _hugely_ appreciated.
