Everything will change with AI. But will we be any better at understanding ourselves? Microsoft AI CEO Mustafa Suleyman and historian and author Yuval Noah Harari agree: AI will create new forms of abundance. But what kinds of abundance, and at what cost? They met in London in August 2026 for a long conversation about abundance, intelligence, consciousness and how technology and AI could impact humankind. This video was produced by the journal The Humanist Review of AI, whose 'Issue 2: Abundance and Scarcity' features an edited transcript of the conversation.
