[Token Pass](https://vercel.com/docs/ai-gateway/token-pass) now includes DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, alongside Kimi K3 and GLM 5.3, each accessed through its preferred provider, with more models to come. The DeepSeek model uses the latest weights (1017).

Token Pass is a $30/month subscription on Pro teams that grants $80 in monthly credit and runs through [AI Gateway](https://vercel.com/ai-gateway). Use it with any coding agent such as [fx](https://fx.sh/), [Pi](https://pi.dev/), [Cline](https://cline.bot/), [Command Code](https://commandcode.ai/) etc. or build your own with the AI SDK, with Zero Data Retention enabled by default.

Add DeepSeek V4.1 Flash from the [Token Pass page](https://vercel.com/d?to=%2F%5Bteam%5D%2F~%2Fai-gateway%2Ftoken-pass%3Fsource%3Dtoken_pass_deepseek_changelog) in your team's AI Gateway dashboard, or read the [Token Pass documentation](https://vercel.com/docs/ai-gateway/token-pass) for setup.
