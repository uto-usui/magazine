Welcome to Source Material, a weekly series where the designers and creatives shaping culture share the references that shaped them. Our next guest is [Ben Lanz](https://officebenganz.com/), the Switzerland-based designer and creative director behind [PIN-UP Magazine](https://www.pinupmagazine.org/).

After the shoot, we caught up with Ben about his creative process:

**What were you making when you realized, “This could be my job”?**

Potato prints in elementary school.

**What’s the first thing you do when you sit down to work?**

Make a to-do list.

**What’s a design object you’d take to your grave?**

My wedding band.

**Whose work makes you jealous?**

[Martin Szekely](https://martinszekely.com/en/).

**What current design trend are you tired of seeing?**

Optimized workflows.

**What rule do you break the most?**

Probably grids?

**What song do you put on to get out of a creative rut?**

[“Als wär's das letzte Mal” by Deutsch Amerikanische Freundschaft (DAF)](https://open.spotify.com/track/76PwvGNMSROsSLSJmRoH8g).

**What’s a piece of feedback you resisted, and later realized was right?**

Innovate as a last resort.

**What’s a part of your process that most people don’t know about?**

There are no shortcuts or secrets.

**What’s the last thing you came across that made you want to go make something?**

A wooden Stabelle chair in my grandma’s chalet.
