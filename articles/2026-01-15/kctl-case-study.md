Kings County Tennis League is a tennis non-profit based in Brooklyn. It’s an incredible organization that brings tennis—the instructors, the equipment, everything—to courts in New York public housing complexes so kids can learn how to play. The team asked us to help them with a rebrand to reflect the emotion people felt when they stepped on court.

While the original brand had done such a great job of getting KCTL where it is today, it felt like it was time to evolve it—making it a little sharper, fresher, and cooler. A lot of the people who loved KCTL’s programming didn’t feel excited to wear their KCTL-branded tees at the park with their friends.

We started by immersing ourselves in their world. We spoke to as many players, instructors, families, and volunteers as we could. We did interviews, went to their parties, and spent time on the courts. We shared two strategic directions with them, and the one they really gravitated towards focused on local pride. Unlike other sports organizations that take kids from their blocks to some fancy facility far away, KCTL brings everything to the kids: instructors, gear, lessons, and tournaments. This deep sense of pride and local Brooklyn roots became a huge inspiration for the whole brand system we built.

For KCTL’s new verbal identity, we developed a voice that mirrored the energy and confidence we heard on court. We kept it conversational without feeling overly casual and helped the team with messaging that made KCTL easier to explain.

The visual identity was also rooted in the brand’s strategy around pride. We looked for inspiration from sports jerseys and teams, the New York City subway, local street photographers, and even old signs people post up for local basketball tournaments. Pride comes through in every design decision, from the colors of each club to the typeface that the NYC subway and NYC parks use.

When we presented the new brand work to their board, the founder said, “I was the most skeptical, and \[now\] I’m the most excited about this work.” The team has been thrilled to bring this brand to life through a new website, new shirts for the players and coaches, and a big 15th anniversary party in the fall.
