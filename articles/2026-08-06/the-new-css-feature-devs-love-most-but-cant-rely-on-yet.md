⚓️ [Results from the 2026 _State of CSS_ Survey](https://2026.stateofcss.com/en-US/ "2026.stateofcss.com") — Developers report _anchor positioning_ as their favorite new CSS feature of this past year, though limited support is holding it back. As for the most-used newer features? `:has()`, `aspect-ratio`, and CSS nesting came out top. The results also touch on AI uptake (or lack thereof), framework adoption, the CSS grid learning curve, and more. Worth a read to get a good sense of the lay of the land.
