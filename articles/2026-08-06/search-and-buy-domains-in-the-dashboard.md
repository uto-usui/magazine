You can now search for and purchase domains from the [Vercel dashboard](https://vercel.com/dashboard).

Open the domain search panel from the Buy button in [domain settings](https://vercel.com/d?to=%2F%5Bteam%5D%2F%5Bproject%5D%2Fsettings%2Fdomains&title=Open+domain+settings) or the + button on the project overview.

Nameservers are configured automatically for your project, so there are no DNS records to set.

Read the [working with domains documentation](https://vercel.com/docs/domains/working-with-domains) to get started.
