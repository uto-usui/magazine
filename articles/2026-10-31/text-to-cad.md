## [Overview](#overview)

The text-to-cad plugin gives your agent local workflows for generating 3D models as STEP, GLB, STL or 3MF files. It also does design for manufacturing checks, generates engineering drawings, and connects to popular 3D printing, sheet metal and CNC fabrication services.

It is supported by all popular agents that support plugins or the [skills](https://skills.sh/) framework, including Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini and Grok.

## [Skills](#skills)

Install the library to give agents focused workflows for CAD, fabrication, robot description files, simulation, and local review.

## [Contributing](#contributing)

text-to-cad is MIT-licensed and built in the open.

-   ### Bugs and plugin requests
    
    A bug report with the model script and the output of `cadgen doctor` is the fastest to fix, and an agent app without a plugin gets one on request. A security problem is reported privately, as SECURITY.md says.
    
    [Open an issue](https://github.com/earthtojake/text-to-cad/issues/new)[Request a plugin](https://github.com/earthtojake/text-to-cad/issues/new?title=Plugin%20request%3A%20)[SECURITY.md](https://github.com/earthtojake/text-to-cad/blob/main/SECURITY.md)
    
-   ### Questions, ideas and what you made
    
    Say what the skills got wrong, too: that is what improves them.
    
    [Join the Discord](https://discord.gg/5FGB9DwJYU)
    
-   ### Code, skills and models
    
    A new skill (a fabrication service, a printer, a CAD workflow) goes in `skills/`, an example model in `models/`, and fixes anywhere. The contributing guide has the local setup and how to test in the agent apps.
    
    [Contributing guide](https://github.com/earthtojake/text-to-cad/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)
