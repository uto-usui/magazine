We have great news. After months of effort, 3 new modules, 13 completely re-worked lessons and 20 _new_ lessons (including practical exercises), JavaScript for Everyone’s New Game+ update is now live!

[

Check it out!

](https://piccalil.li/javascript-for-everyone?utm_source=piccalilli-launch-link&utm_medium=author-promo)

We took feedback from the community about what they thought was missing from the course and overwhelmingly, people wanted practical exercises. It makes sense: JavaScript is a complex language to learn and there’s not many better ways to cement new knowledge than by actually building with it.

Mat took this feedback as a challenge, formulated a plan and _got to work_, disappearing into a text editor and reappearing with pretty much a whole course’s worth of new and updated material.

It’s really inspiring to me, personally, because doing _anything_ while it felt like our industry was being decimated felt impossible. Mat’s resolve to keep doing what he does well: _teach_ people, has been unmatched and the benefactors of that effort are _everyone_.

JavaScript for Everyone was already _the_ canonical, high quality educational resource for learning JavaScript, but now, it’s at a whole new level.

So what are you waiting for? Take it for a spin and truly understand how JavaScript works!
