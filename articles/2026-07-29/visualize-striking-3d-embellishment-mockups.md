## What is the DREAMCOMPOSER® software?

DREAMCOMPOSER® is a web-based application that visualizes high-quality packaging designs in 3D. Packaging, bottles, mascara tubes, jars or individual shapes can be decorated virtually. In just a few steps, a PDF document is transformed into a fully designed 3D model.

DREAMCOMPOSER® offers a selection of over 100 transfer decorations with brilliant effects from LEONHARD KURZ that will turn your packaging into real eye-catchers.
