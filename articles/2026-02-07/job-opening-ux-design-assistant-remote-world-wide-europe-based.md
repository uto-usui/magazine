**Job Title**  
UX Design Assistant

**Application Deadline**  
February 6, 2026

**Location**  
Europe

## About NN/g

NNGroup is a **UX insights organization** trusted by leading organizations world-wide to provide reliable guidance on user experience. We provide evidence-based UX articles, in-depth training, research reports, and consulting. 

We’re a small, **fully remote** company (fewer than 40 people). We believe deeply in **our mission to help the world learn about and improve UX.**  
 

## Role Description

NNGroup is looking for a talented UX design assistant to join the team. As a UX design assistant, you will work alongside our UX designers to improve the NN/G website experience, as well as other internal and client-facing experiences. This individual must have strong UX design skills, some knowledge of visual design skills, and a high capacity to learn.  
 

## Responsibilities

-   Assist in UX design work to improve the NN/G website
-   Spec design work for the development team to implement designs
-   Review implemented designs and provide feedback to developers
-   Create high-fidelity prototypes using Figma for use in usability testing studies

## Required Qualifications

-   Undergraduate or graduate student currently enrolled at a university or college pursuing a degree in visual design, graphic design, interaction design, or a related field. Expected graduation date of December 2027 or later
-   Strong UX design skills
-   Adequate visual design skills
-   Proficient using Figma
-   Able to solve problems and quickly adapt to feedback
-   Speak fluent English with advanced business proficiency
-   Work overlap with US eastern and CET (France) timezones by 4 hours

## Compensation & Perks

Base pay is just one component of NN/G’s total compensation. Position benefits include:

-   Hourly base pay of € 8-15 Euro, dependent on location and experience
-   Attend UX trainings and receive NN/G UX Certification (valued at $7,000)
-   Fully remote position
-   Flexible schedule to accommodate rigorous demands of school schedule (10-30 hours per week)
-   Software expenses fully covered
-   Team of mentors committed to your success
