**Job Title**  
Social Media Design Assistant

**Application Deadline**  
February 6, 2026

**Location**  
Europe

## About NN/g

NNGroup is a **UX insights organization** trusted by leading organizations world-wide to provide reliable guidance on user experience. We provide evidence-based UX articles, in-depth training, research reports, and consulting. 

We’re a small, **fully remote** company (fewer than 40 people). We believe deeply in **our mission to help the world learn about and improve UX.**  
 

## Role Description

NNGroup is looking for a talented social media design assistant to join the team. As a social media design assistant, you will work alongside the design and social teams to communicate complex UX topics through videos, visuals and thumbnails and complete tasks that help strengthen NN/G’s brand identity. This individual must have strong video editing and visual design skills, some knowledge of UX and a high capacity to learn.  
 

## Responsibilities

-   Creating weekly social media imagery and posts
-   Editing YouTube videos and shorts, and Instagram reels
-   Assisting with social media planning

## Required Qualifications

-   Undergraduate or graduate student currently enrolled at a university or college pursuing a degree in digital marketing, marketing, communication, design or a related field. Expected graduation date of December 2027 or later
-   Strong video editing and visual design skills
-   Proficient using Premiere Pro and After Effects
-   Comfortable using Figma (or willingness to learn)
-   Able to translate article content into easy-to-understand social media posts
-   Able to solve problems and quickly adapt to feedback
-   Speak fluent English with advanced business proficiency
-   Work overlap with US eastern and CET timezones by 4 hours

Base pay is just one component of NN/G’s total compensation. Position benefits include:

-   Hourly base pay of € 8-15 Euro, dependent on location and experience
-   Attend UX trainings and receive NN/G UX Certification (valued at $7,000)
-   Fully remote position
-   Flexible schedule to accommodate rigorous demands of school schedule (10-30 hours per week)
-   Software expenses fully covered
-   Team of mentors committed to your success
