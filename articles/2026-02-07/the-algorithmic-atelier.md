> **[Fall in love with your new reality](https://alearningaday.blog/2025/12/27/fall-in-love-with-your-new-reality/?ref=sidebar) →**  
> “Sometimes our role in life isn’t to judge something – it is to figure out how to fall in love with it, especially when it becomes part of our new reality. The other day, I was in a conversation where someone was describing their new commute. The question that came up was, ‘Do you like it?’ And of course, the natural instinct is to evaluate it: I don’t like this part, I don’t like that part, etc.”

> **[How to design for (and with) Deaf people](https://www.smashingmagazine.com/2025/12/how-design-for-with-deaf-people/?ref=sidebar) →**  
> “Deafness spans a broad continuum, from minor to profound hearing loss. Around 90–95% of deaf people come from hearing families, and deafness often isn’t merely a condition that people are born with. It frequently occurs due to exposure to loud noises, and it also emerges with age, disease, and accidents.”
