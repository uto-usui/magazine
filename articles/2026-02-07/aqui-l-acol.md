In “Aqui Lá Acolá”, photographer Arthur Petrillo (they/them) traces a poetic cartography of belonging across the UK, Brasil, and Japan. Each landscape reflects a different stage of self: the present home, the origin, and the distant mirror. The images are an invitation into an intimate exploration of identity, displacement, and the evolving perception shaped by space, time and Arthur’s own gender journey.

I feel the echo of the expectations that shaped me. The absence of space to simply be. Inspired by Milton Nascimento, I walk with my camera to reclaim my story. Memory into presence. Erasure into voice.

Rewriting the narrative

It’s in the furthest place that I turn inwards. Silence and observation guide me toward balance. Tradition and change. Solitude and connection, Wherever I go, I am my own home.
