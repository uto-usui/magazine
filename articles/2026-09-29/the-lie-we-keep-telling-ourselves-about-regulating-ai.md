“Congress is still writing in waterfall, stuck in a world that is quickly passing them by. It wants one comprehensive (dare I say beautiful) AI bill, fully specified, passed once, ideally after everyone agrees, which is to say never.

The internet’s builders would have found that laughable. They governed a technology that was changing under their feet, and they did it by treating governance the way good teams treat a product, as a living thing you steer, not a monument you unveil.”
