In 2000 a typical mobile phone was about a quarter metal by weight. Most of the rest was plastic, with some ceramics. In 2016 the U.S. Geological Survey listed examples of the mined materials that go into a mobile device and the parts that use them. This page takes that list, adds cobalt for batteries that use it, and shows which countries produced each material in 2025.

Prompted by Jussi Parikka’s _A Geology of Media_ (University of Minnesota Press, 2015), which starts the history of media with the minerals they are made from.
