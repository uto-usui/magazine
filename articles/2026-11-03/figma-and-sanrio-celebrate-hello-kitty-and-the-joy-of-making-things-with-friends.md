Figma, the full-stack creative canvas, and Sanrio®, the global lifestyle brand behind pop culture icon Hello Kitty®, are coming together to celebrate a simple idea: making things is better with friends. For one week starting on November 1, Hello Kitty’s birthday, Sanrio’s cast of characters are finding a home in FigJam, Figma’s online whiteboard for collaboration, bringing a little dash of kawaii to brainstorms, group projects, and everything in between. On November 6-8, the party moves offline with a themed photobooth and free pop-up in New York City featuring limited-edition gifts and surprises.

This marks [Sanrio’s first integration with Figma](https://www.figma.com/hello-kitty-and-friends/?utm_source=press&utm_medium=web&utm_campaign=hello+kitty+birthday), coming at a time when team spirit and imagination matter more than ever. The two companies share a commitment to community: Figma is home to one of the most creative communities on the internet, while Hello Kitty and Friends are centered around friendship, supporting one another, and spreading kindness across generations. Both believe the best work happens together, with plenty of room for play.

“Figma is a place for collaboration and expression, with room for every idea and every tool you need to bring it to life. Sanrio’s beloved characters will feel right at home in Figma, surrounded by a community that cares just as much about craft and creativity,” said Nairi Hourdajian, Chief Marketing Officer at Figma. “This collab is a little nostalgic, a lot of fun, and a reminder that making things is even better with friends by your side.”

“Hello Kitty and Friends have always been about friendship, creativity, and bringing people together,” said Jill Koch, Chief Brand Officer, Sanrio, Inc. “Our collaboration with Figma brings that spirit to a community that shares our love of creativity and self-expression. We’re excited to see how people incorporate their favorite characters into their work, connect with others, and bring a little more joy to the creative process.”

Here’s how to join the fun over the next week:

## [Collaborate with Hello Kitty and Friends in FigJam](#collaborate-with-hello-kitty-and-friends-in-figjam)

FigJam is an online whiteboard where friends come together for work or fun, from ice breakers and greeting cards to team meetings and party plans. Through November 8, you can select from 12 Sanrio friends in FigJam, including Hello Kitty, Pochacco™, My Melody™, Kuromi™, Cinnamoroll™, Pompompurin™, and more. Choose a character to transform the FigJam experience with supercute cursors, themed washi tape, colorful sticky notes, and photobooth selfie frames. Keep jamming to discover more Hello Kitty and Friends surprises.

Figma’s designers worked closely with Sanrio to bring each character’s palette and personality [into the product](https://www.figma.com/blog/hello-kitty-and-friends-in-figma/)

, so every detail feels delightful without getting in the way of real work. FigJam has always encouraged imagination with tools to express yourself like stamps, emotes, high-fives, and built-in tunes to stay in the flow. With Hello Kitty and Friends cheering you on from the toolbar, you never have to jam alone.

Hello Kitty and Friends are also joining the Figma Community, where creatives connect and share their work with each other, including design templates, skills, and even games. For one week, you can personalize your community profile by featuring your top three characters and selecting a Sanrio-themed background. Browse the community to find FigJam tips and tricks in a new [Sanrio playground file](https://www.figma.com/community/file/1695515201660847042), design [coloring book pages](https://www.figma.com/community/file/1698704255287063696), and bring smiles to your next presentation with an adorable [Figma Slides template](https://www.figma.com/community/file/1698050154151729793).

## [A birthday party in NYC](#a-birthday-party-in-nyc)

The celebration is moving IRL. Over the weekend, Figma and Sanrio are throwing a public [party for Hello Kitty](https://therumor.com/p/figmaisbetterwithfriends?ref=kdoh0w15) near Union Square in New York City. Enjoy an insta-worthy matcha or strawberry milk at a green Hello Kitty and Friends truck before heading inside to collect a mystery character keychain and customize it at the charm bar, then pose for a photo opp with your new friend. More party favors await, including a limited-edition sweatshirt and patches for the first 150 visitors each day.

Leading up to the bash, swing by the Old Friend photobooth in the Lower East Side from November 6-7 for a custom photostrip and invitation to the weekend pop-up. Keep an eye out for tearaway posters around the city for another take-home souvenir.

## [Celebrating friends around the world](#celebrating-friends-around-the-world)

The fun doesn’t stop in New York. A global network of Friends of Figma community groups and Figma Campus Leaders are hosting more than 60 Hello Kitty and Friends-themed meetups in cities and on campuses around the world. Bringing Sanrio fans and Figma aficionados together to connect, play, and make new friends, these gatherings might just be the cutest way to network. Find local community events at [friends.figma.com](https://friends.figma.com/).

Figma and Sanrio hope this collaboration will inspire friends to create together and bring a bit of play to their everyday lives. Mark your calendar:

-   **Hello Kitty and Friends are** **available in FigJam** from November 1-8. Get started [here](https://www.figma.com/hello-kitty-and-friends/?utm_source=press&utm_medium=web&utm_campaign=hello+kitty+birthday).
-   Visit the **Old Friend photobooth** for a free Figma and Sanrio themed photostrip from November 6-7, 10:00AM-10:00PM ET at 145 Allen Street, New York.
-   [**Hello Kitty’s birthday party pop-up**](https://therumor.com/p/figmaisbetterwithfriends?ref=kdoh0w15) is November 7-8, 10:00AM-6:00PM ET at 12 East 14th Street, New York. First come, first served!

**About Sanrio**

Sanrio is the global lifestyle brand best known for pop icon Hello Kitty®, and home to many other beloved character brands including Chococat™, My Melody™, Badtz-maru™, Keroppi™, Gudetama™ and Aggretsuko™. Sanrio was founded on the “Small Gift, Big Smile®” philosophy – that a small gift can bring happiness and friendship to people of all ages. Since 1960, this philosophy has served as the inspiration to offer quality products, services and activities that inspire unique consumer experiences across the world. Today, Sanrio's legendary breadth of products are available in over 130 countries and in retail locations including department, specialty, national chain retailers and Sanrio boutique stores. To learn more about Sanrio, please visit www.sanrio.com and follow @sanrio and @hellokitty on Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, TikTok, Pinterest and subscribe to the Hello Kitty and Friends YouTube Channel.
