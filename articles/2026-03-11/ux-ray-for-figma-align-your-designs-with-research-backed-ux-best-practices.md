## How To Use UX-Ray To Instantly Spot & Fix UX Issues In Figma Designs

**Step 1:** [Install the UX-Ray Figma plugin](https://www.figma.com/community/plugin/1608088797036191266/ux-ray-by-baymard).

**Step 2:** Open your Figma desktop app and navigate to “Plugins” on the top nav bar, click UX-Ray by Baymard.

**Step 3:** Select a design frame and click scan.

**Step 4:** Browse through the list of UX insights.

**Step 5:** Click on a UX insight to see an explanation of each UI’s component implementation and how to fix it or why it’s good UX.

**Step 6:** For competitor analysis, add a screenshot of competitor pages, select the frame and click scan.

### How to Interpret the Ratings

Each rating has a [**95% accuracy rate**](https://baymard.com/blog/ai-heuristic-evaluations) when compared to Baymard’s human auditors.

We do not allow UX-Ray to publish ratings below the 95% threshold **to ensure** UX analysis and CRO suggestions do not harm conversions.

Below are the **types of ratings** you will receive based on the observed user behavior in Baymard’s large-scale testing:

**“UX issues”** are UX implementations identified that don’t align with Baymard’s research-backed UX Best Practice Guidelines.

We suggest **taking action** to change these implementations.

There are **4 types** of UX issue ratings that vary in severity of observed user friction and task abandonment:

-   **Very Big Issue:** Severe UX issue that leads to task or site abandonment for most to all users.
-   **Big Issue:** Severe UX issue that leads to task or site abandonment for some to most users.
-   **Medium Issue:** Moderate UX issue that needs troubleshooting from some to most users.
-   **Small Issue:** Minor UX issue that briefly interrupts users.

**“Good”** are UX implementations identified that follow [Baymard’s UX Best Practice Guidelines](https://baymard.com/product/ux-best-practice-guidelines); we suggest keeping these UX implementations as they enable users to complete tasks and adhere to a user’s expectations.

**“Unrated”** are UX Guidelines that we identified as relevant to your design but have not been automatically rated by UX-Ray.

We **suggest** manually reviewing these insights and implementing the recommended best practice.

**“Not Applicable”** are UX Best Practice Guidelines we have that do not apply to the specific scanned design frame.
