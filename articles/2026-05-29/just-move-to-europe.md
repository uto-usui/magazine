## Europe basics before you land

The boring stuff that saves the first week. Pick a city when you need the local version.

-   **Visa:**Rules are country-specific. UK and Switzerland are separate from the EU. Pick a city, then follow that city's visa section.
-   **Banking:**Cards work almost everywhere. Have Wise or Revolut anyway for multi-currency, low fees, and fast setup.
-   **SIM:**Get a local number on day 1. Many landlords only respond to local numbers.
-   **Plug:**EU Type C (two round pins). UK uses Type G. Bring adapters.
-   **Culture:**Most useful networking happens in repeated rooms, not one-off coffee spam. Show up twice.

The cold-start message

"Hey \[name\], I just moved to \[city\] and I'm building \[one sentence\]. I'd love to hear how you got started here. Coffee this week?"

Keep it short.

Use these when the city page is too narrow: cross-border funding, legal setup, hiring, and tools.

Europe is not one startup market, which is the point. You can pick your operating base for runway, visa path, talent, weather, or deep-tech access, then travel into the rest of the network.

The trick is not pretending London, Berlin, Lisbon, Tallinn, and Zurich solve the same problem. Pick the constraint that matters for your next six months.

The upside is optionality: better burn, real public support, dense technical universities, and enough builder rooms to build without copying someone else's default.
