The [new episode](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YFEvELx7HcE&list=PLRVyFmnlMfe0lMYaPF6zcT2JVUdpAjg9-&index=1) of [Wake Up Excited!](https://wakeupexcited.show/) features the ultra-talented and ultra-enthusiastic [Rebecca Garza Bortman](https://www.rebeccabortman.com/)!

We have a fantastic conversation where we discuss the importance of family, how music and the arts can make you immortal, her and her husband’s rock-opera wedding, exuberance as fuel, the power of play in life and in work, the power of the stories we tell ourselves and as a society, and how everyone can cultivate their creative capacity.

If you’re into creative and inspiring conversations, I’d love for you to [subscribe to the show](https://podcast.wakeupexcited.show/subscribe)!
