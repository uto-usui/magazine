2025 was another busy year for ResearchOps, both us as a community and Research Operations as a practice.

As with other professions, AI has been a presence in the industry, both as one that can help or perhaps replace us as budget cuts and reductions in workforce hit ReOps.

The articles, webinars and interviews we hosted have reflected how ResearchOps is working to continue to prove its value with strategies and tools even as the industry tightens: how to work with AI, demonstrating the value of ReOps, and some wonderful case studies and examples of research in action.

### Medium articles

As always we were fortunate to have a range of voices from across the globe contribute to our publication and share their experience and thoughts. We’ve grouped 2025 articles into three themes: how ReOps adds value; working with AI; and case studies of working in ReOps.

### ResearchOps adds value

Research operations has been a target for cuts, often as the way that organisation operate it can be tricky to align outputs with organisational value. We spent time talking about how ReOps is not a “cost centre” but a “value centre” that adds information and insight.

-   [**The first cut is to ResearchOps, and how to avoid it**](https://medium.com/researchops-community/the-first-cut-is-to-researchops-and-how-to-avoid-it-with-kate-towsey-4b86eaccb5b9) — Kate Towsey talked about her experience at Atlassian on how to make your impact so visible that they can’t afford to let you go.
-   [**Adapting the Value Proposition Canvas to Communicate Research Value**](https://medium.com/researchops-community/adapting-the-value-proposition-canvas-to-communicate-research-value-a3fdbd48a614) — Dawn Ta took a marketing tool and adapted it to explain what we do in a language that stakeholders actually understand.
-   [**Setting up a ResearchOps capability — Lessons learnt**](https://medium.com/@voula.gkatzidou/setting-up-s-researchops-capability-lessons-learnt-4efb961398e5) — Voula Gkatzidou shared her journey of building a ResearchOps, including “I wish I had known that” moments.

### Does AI have a place in ResearchOps?

AI has gone from a fun if flawed tool to something that has rapidly spread throughout the broader UX industry, including ReOps. We have some practical advice on using it in research operations.

-   [**The future of research ops: AI as a role-redefiner**](https://medium.com/researchops-community/the-future-of-research-ops-with-kate-towsey-ai-wont-replace-researchers-but-will-redefine-7c9ff9c50d05) — Part 2 of our interview with Kate Towsey on how AI can handle the drudgery so we can focus on strategy and operations.
-   [**Using AI Tools to Augment User Experience Research Workflows**](https://medium.com/researchops-community/using-ai-tools-to-augment-user-experience-research-workflows-b10e9bddec9a) — Adam Malamis broke down six ways to use these tools right now, from planning to sentiment analysis, without losing the “human” in human-centered design.
-   [**AI-Augmented Workflows: Making Insights Actionable**](https://medium.com/researchops-community/ai-augmented-workflows-for-ux-research-making-insights-accessible-and-actionable-7b6f7ee6aebc) — Nathaniel Steinrueck argued that AI in ReOps can go beyond better recruitment of participants but can also surface existing research to ensure it’s not forgotten.

### Practical experience of researchops

While AI is the current fad, the day-to-day work of finding the right people and mapping complex systems is still where most of our energy goes. This year, we saw some brilliant examples of the hard parts of ReOps.

Cases studies highlight how different researchops professionals operate, and this year we had a global series of examples:

-   [**Research as a Product**](https://medium.com/researchops-community/research-as-a-product-building-sustainable-relationship-driven-research-programs-cab458a1d882) — Prama Ayalasomayajula shared an insight she had about researchops: stop thinking about one-off projects and start building relationships
-   [**Loving the “Impossible” Operational Problems**](https://medium.com/researchops-community/how-i-learned-to-stop-worrying-and-love-the-difficult-operational-problems-3661e407105d) — What happens when a well-intentioned research project seems to end in disaster? Prama show us how to fall in love with the ReOps problems and position yourself as a valued team collaborator.
-   [**A Framework for Multi-Actor B2B Journeys**](https://medium.com/researchops-community/a-practical-framework-for-multi-actor-b2b-customer-journeys-8b7d44d77c74) — Daniela Nguyen Trong shares a step-by-step guide for mapping complex B2B ecosystems across multiple countries.
-   [**Designing Recruitment Posters That Actually Work**](https://medium.com/researchops-community/) — Sometimes the most effective solution for recruitment is the simplest one: a poster or flyer posted in the right place. Written by Ariel Wang.
