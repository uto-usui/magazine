Well here we are at the end of another year, and here's my own review of what happened this year in my life.

## [Epic things](#epic-things)

The biggest thing that happened professionally this year was the launch of [Epic Web](https://www.epicweb.dev/) and the [Epic Stack](https://github.com/epicweb-dev/epic-stack). Those required their own post, so I wrote that up separately and you can read all about how that came to be in [Launching Epic Web](https://kentcdodds.com/blog/launching-epic-web).

This was really a big deal for me!

## [Conferences](#conferences)

![Kent standing on stage](https://res.cloudinary.com/kentcdodds-com/image/upload/f_auto,q_auto,dpr_2.0,w_1600/kentcdodds.com/content/blog/2023-in-review/kent-speaking)

You can find my travel history (and future) on my [travel map](https://kcd.im/map). In 2023, I spoke at the following conferences:

-   [Agent Conf](https://agent.sh/) in Dornbirn, Austria
-   [QCon London](https://qconlondon.com/) in London, England
-   [CityJS London](https://london.cityjsconf.org/) in London, England
-   [React Miami](https://react-miami.com/) in Miami, Florida
-   [Remix Conf](https://remix.run/conf) in Salt Lake City, Utah
-   [JSDay Canarias](https://jsdaycanarias.com/) in Las Palmas de Gran Canaria, Spain
-   [React Rally](https://www.reactrally.com/) in Salt Lake City, Utah
-   [UtahJS Conf](https://conf.utahjs.com/) in Salt Lake City, Utah
-   [React Live](https://reactlive.nl/) in Amsterdam, Netherlands

Pretty busy year for conferences for me. My wife and I had our 5th child in November though, so I'm easing off a little bit for 2024. I've still got some pretty sweet plans though. Stay tuned.

I want to call out a couple things though.

First, Agent Conf was unbelievable. Aside from the organizer ([Guntram](https://x.com/guntrambechtold)) just being a stellar person who put together an amazing event. Skiing in the Alps was unforgettable (though I must say I'm partial to Utah snow).

I also had a great time at CityJS and really enjoyed spending **6 hours** at Harry Potter and the Cursed Child part 1 and 2 back-to-back with my friends [Ruben Casas](https://x.com/Infoxicador) and [Adrien Baron](https://x.com/BaronAdri).

## [Personal](#personal)

The biggest thing that happened in my personal life this year was the birth of my 5th child. I don't post pictures of my kids online, but I will say that I'm very grateful for my wife and kids. They're the best. I'm so grateful my wife and my new son are healthy and happy!

Earlier this year we took our kids to Disneyland for the first time. It was fantastic. My oldest daughter is 11 and she's willing to go on almost all the rides with me (except for Guardians of the Galaxy 😅). We had an awesome time (Rise of the Resistance is incredible).

My wife made a cake for me in the theme of Epic Web. She's amazing.

## [Conclusion](#conclusion)

It's cool to look back on 2023 and think of all the good times. I love looking through pictures of the experiences I had with family and friends. The older I get, the more I come to appreciate the relationships I have with other people.

As the world gets somehow even more digital, I'm clinging to these real human relationships harder than ever and I hope you are too. I'm convinced that the greatest happiness is only achieved by cultivating meaningful relationships with other people.

So, onto 2024!
