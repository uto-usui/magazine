For years, India’s design and developer community has pushed the boundaries of what’s possible in Figma. Now, with our new hub in Bengaluru, we are excited to meet these builders where they are—and learn firsthand how they’re reimagining what’s next and shaping the future of product development in India.

###### Key insights from our India research:

• 93% of designers say their companies already use AI in design.

• 85% of designers agree that AI simplifies writing code.

• 81% of developers say design has become more important to the success of AI-powered products.

More than 7,000 people registered for the launch event, joining us to celebrate the Bengaluru office opening on November 12, 2025 and learn more about our plans for India. As part of this event, we shared the latest in AI design tools and new research into how AI is changing the way people are designing and building in India.1

“Today India is our second-largest market by monthly active users. As it evolves into a global software and manufacturing hub, the value of design will only grow,” says Scott Pugh, APAC Vice President of Sales at Figma. “We’ve seen increasing demand for modern design tools from Indian companies, and with the office opening, we want to get closer to our users and the community here.”

## [Building with India’s most ambitious teams](#building-with-india-s-most-ambitious-teams)

From startups to some of the country’s biggest brands—like Airtel, [CARS24](https://www.figma.com/customers/driving-design-transformation-how-cars24-builds-faster-with-figma/), [Flipkart](https://www.figma.com/customers/how-flipkart-boosts-its-design-vision-for-indian-e-commerce-with-figma/), [Groww](https://www.figma.com/customers/figma-helps-groww-simplify-investing/), [Juspay](https://www.figma.com/customer/from-design-to-code-how-juspay-achieved-5x-faster-workflows-with-figma/), Myntra, [Swiggy](https://www.figma.com/customers/how-swiggy-rolls-out-features-50-percent-faster/), TCS and Zomato—teams are using Figma to design, build, and launch faster than ever.

As of September 2025, over 40% of the Bombay Stock Exchange 100 (BSE100) companies use Figma, and our [Friends of Figma community](https://friends.figma.com/chapters/) includes close to 25,000 members across the country, in cities from Ahmedabad to Vadodara.

“Design is at the heart of everything we do—and Figma has become an integral part of how we bring that vision to life,” says Mr. Lakshminarayan Swaminathan, Vice President of Product Management and Design at Myntra, one of India’s largest online retailers. “It unites our teams around a shared creative vision, accelerates idea-to-product, and keeps us at the forefront of evolving digital experiences, especially with AI… we look forward to deepening our partnership in shaping the future of design.”

## [From idea to product—with craft and consistency](#from-idea-to-product-with-craft-and-consistency)

###### Figma’s global community

8​5​% of Figma users are based outside the United States, as of Q1 2025​. In 2024, about half of Figma’s revenue came from markets outside the US.​ The company has accelerated its global footprint ​in recent ​years, opening offices in ​New York, ​Tokyo, Singapore, London, Paris, Berlin, Sydney, São Paulo, and now, Bengaluru.

At the launch, we shared updates across Figma’s expanding product suite—including our recent acquisition of [Weavy](https://www.figma.com/blog/welcome-weavy-to-figma/)

, a generative AI platform, and new features like [design systems integration](https://www.figma.com/blog/schema-2025-design-systems-recap/)

in Figma Make. These updates will help teams scale their work across the full platform, which is now available to all users in India and includes:

-   [Figma Design](https://www.figma.com/design/): Design and build digital products
-   [Figma Make](https://www.figma.com/make/): An AI-powered tool to turn prompts or designs into prototypes and apps
-   [Dev Mode](https://www.figma.com/dev-mode/): A workspace for developers to translate designs into code
-   [Figma Sites](https://www.figma.com/sites/): Design and publish dynamic websites
-   [FigJam](https://www.figma.com/figjam/): An online whiteboard to brainstorm and collaborate
-   [Figma Slides](https://www.figma.com/slides/): Create and deliver interactive presentations
-   [Figma Draw](https://www.figma.com/draw/): Illustrate with advanced vector tools
-   [Figma Buzz](https://www.figma.com/buzz/): Help marketing teams produce on-brand assets at scale

Figma has evolved from a design tool to an AI-powered platform that enables collaboration across the product development process—from early-stage ideation to development and code.

1 Research conducted with YouGov, surveying 730 professionals working in leadership roles in design, engineering and product.
