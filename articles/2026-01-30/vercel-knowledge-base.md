Vercel [Knowledge Base](https://vercel.com/kb) is a new home for guides, tutorials, and best practices for developers building on Vercel, including how to:

-   [Build agentic apps with the AI cloud](https://vercel.com/kb/ai)
    
-   [Host your Backend with your preferred framework](https://vercel.com/kb/backend)
    
-   [Secure your apps with Vercel's built-in features](https://vercel.com/kb/security)

You can use the Knowledge Base to find and explore guides for specific use cases with:

-   **Semantic AI search**: describe what you're trying to achieve
    
-   **AI chat**: ask our agent about a guide
    
-   **Filters**: search guides by Vercel product or feature
