Pro and Enterprise teams can now use Static IPs to securely connect to external services like databases that require IP allowlisting. Traffic from builds and functions routes through consistent, shared static IPs.

To enable Static IPs, you can access [Connectivity > Static IPs](https://vercel.com/d?to=%2F%5Bteam%5D%2F%7E%2Fsettings%2Fconnectivity&title=Go+to+Connectivity+Settings) from your project or team settings.

Static IPs are offered in addition to [Secure Compute](https://vercel.com/docs/connectivity/secure-compute), which remains available for teams that need a fully dedicated VPC model. Note, Secure Compute has also moved within the Connectivity settings of your projects and teams.

This is part of our move to [bring more enterprise features self-serve](https://vercel.com/blog/new-pro-pricing-plan).

Read the [docs](https://vercel.com/docs/connectivity/static-ips) or [enable Static IPs here](https://vercel.com/d?to=%2F%5Bteam%5D%2F%7E%2Fsettings%2Fconnectivity&title=Go+to+Connectivity+Settings).
