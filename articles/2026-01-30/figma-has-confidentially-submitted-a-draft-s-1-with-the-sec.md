April 15, 2025

Today, we’re sharing that Figma has confidentially submitted an S-1 with the Securities and Exchange Commission, giving us the option to go public, pending SEC review.

_This blog post is being made in accordance with Rule 135 under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the Securities Act). The fine print:_

Figma, Inc. has confidentially submitted a draft Registration Statement on Form S-1 with the SEC relating to the proposed initial public offering of its Class A common stock. This is not an offer to sell or solicitation of an offer to buy any securities. The number of shares and the price range for a potential public offering have not yet been determined. Any future offers, solicitations or offers to buy, or sales of securities will be made following the SEC’s completion of its review process, in accordance with the registration requirements of the Securities Act.
