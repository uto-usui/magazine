[The 2025 Web Almanac](https://frontendfoc.us/link/179539/web "almanac.httparchive.org") — This always interesting, detailed, annual report returns, having analyzed over 17 million websites and processed some 244TB of data! It highlights how the web has evolved over the past year — told via chapters on accessibility, performance, security, content distribution, AI and more. “_We hope our report helps the community understand the current state of the web better_”.

TimescaleDB gives you hypertables for automatic partitioning, 95% compression, and continuous aggregates that refresh in real-time. Full Postgres compatibility. Tiger Data (creators of TimescaleDB)

[jQuery 4.0 Released](https://frontendfoc.us/link/179540/web "blog.jquery.com") — This major release comes [20 years on](https://frontendfoc.us/link/179541/web) from the library's debut! Version 4.0 drops support for IE 10 and older, adds in support for Trusted Types, aligns with the W3C spec on focus events, and most notably sees a full migration to ES modules — meaning it’s compatible with modern build tools!

-   📖 After surveying the publishing community last year, it's been decided that [HTML will not be added](https://frontendfoc.us/link/179546/web) to the EPUB format.
    
-   🌐 Mark Nottingham shared his thoughts on [the importance of the open web](https://frontendfoc.us/link/179548/web).
    
-   🎂 Wikipedia has been with us for a quarter of a century. [Here's a special site to celebrate](https://frontendfoc.us/link/179549/web) the 25 year milestone.
