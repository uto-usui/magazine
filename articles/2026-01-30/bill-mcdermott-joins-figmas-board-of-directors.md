July 1, 2025

Bill McDermott, the Chairman and CEO of ServiceNow, is joining Figma’s Board of Directors.

Bill McDermott, the Chairman and CEO of ServiceNow, is joining Figma’s Board of Directors. As a proven leader with deep experience scaling and selling to large enterprise companies, we believe his addition to our board will support Figma’s continued growth.

“Bill’s story is legendary—from operating a deli at 17, to selling Xerox machines to leading SAP and ServiceNow through incredible growth, his experience operating at scale is unmatched,” said Dylan Field, co-founder and CEO of Figma. “As we got to know each other, I especially appreciated his humility and authenticity in addition to his wisdom. We’re excited to learn from him.”

As CEO of ServiceNow, Bill has spearheaded the company's remarkable expansion, solidifying its position as the AI platform for business transformation. In his prior role as CEO of SAP, he showcased his ability to build global business ecosystems. Under Bill's leadership, ServiceNow and SAP each experienced significant growth—with both companies' market caps more than tripling during his respective tenures. Both ServiceNow and SAP are Figma customers.

Other members of Figma’s Board (in alphabetical order) include:

-   Dylan Field, co-founder and CEO of Figma (Chair)
-   Mamoon Hamid, Managing Member at Kleiner Perkins
-   Kelly Kramer, former Executive Vice President and CFO of Cisco Systems
-   John Lilly, General Partner at Greylock and former CEO of Mozilla
-   Andrew Reed, partner at Sequoia
-   Danny Rimer, partner at Index Ventures
-   Lynn Vojvodich Radakovich, former Executive Vice President and CMO of Salesforce

**About Figma**
