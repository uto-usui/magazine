Pro teams can now access a [new usage dashboard](https://vercel.com/d?to=%2F%5Bteam%5D%2F%7E%2Fusage&title=Go+to+usage+page) (recently [introduced to Enterprise customers](https://vercel.com/changelog/new-usage-dashboard-for-enterprise-users)) with improved filtering, detailed breakdowns, and export options to better understand usage and costs by product and project.

You can now break down usage by:

-   **Product** to quickly identify usage, drill down into spikes, and track costs of a single or set of products
    
-   **Team and project** to understand your costs and monitor team activity across all or specific apps
    
-   **CSV exports** for external analysis via integration into your cost observability tools and spreadsheets
    

Explore the [new dashboard](https://vercel.com/d?to=%2F%5Bteam%5D%2F%7E%2Fusage&title=Go%20to%20the%20new%20usage%20dashboard) today.
