Design systems are no longer just pattern libraries and sticker sheets, but a [shared language](https://www.figma.com/blog/config-europe-2020-new-feature-announcements/)

that embraces both design and code counterparts. In doing so, they enable the freeform exploration of design while also powering the rigor and structure of code. In addition to helping teams move faster and more efficiently, design systems provide consistency and a single source of truth for your team.

But figuring out how to architect and implement a design system can be daunting. Whether it’s migrating your existing design system, scaling or optimizing a new system, or improving your design-to-development workflows, we want to make sure that you and your organization have the support you need.

> Figma has unlocked our ability to cocreate with our partners, giving our team the ability to move at the pace of the conversation in a platform that is as fast as it is easy to navigate. We are excited to partner with Figma to realize the true potential of collaboration, efficiency, and organization with our clients” – Matt Faulk, CEO at BASIC®

## [How our agency partners can help](#how-our-agency-partners-can-help)

Since our partners have experience working with organizations of all sizes, and across industries, they can provide end-to-end support as you migrate, set up, and scale your design system.

### [Setting up your design system](#setting-up-your-design-system)

If you’re looking to migrate your design tool stack, our partners will get you up and running in Figma. Even if you already use Figma, they can provide best practices for structuring a design system to meet the specific needs of your brand and team.

### [Improving processes](#improving-processes)

Migrating or building out a design systems is just the first step, especially as design becomes a more strategic partner across your organization. But how your team uses and maintains a design system is just as important as its structure. Our partners can establish guiding principles, recommend processes, and provide a framework for improving the workflow between development and design.

### [Defining and scaling your brand](#defining-and-scaling-your-brand)

While design systems will help your team stay on the same page, at their core, they’re about how your brand translates to designs. In addition to best practices on structure, processes, and implementation, our partners can also offer more strategic guidance on how to bring your brand to life.

> "Figma has fundamentally changed how we approach innovation alongside our clients. We've seen firsthand how gaps in communication—across tools or teams—can introduce inconsistencies and inefficiencies. Figma puts us on the same page as our clients (literally), allowing us to prioritise what's truly important for their success: collaboration, transparency, and velocity." – Jack Clark, Lead Product Designer at Idean
