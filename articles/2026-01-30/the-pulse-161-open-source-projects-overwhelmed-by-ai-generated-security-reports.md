_The Pulse is a series covering events, insights, and trends within Big Tech and startups. Notice an interesting event or trend? Hit reply and share it with me._

Today, we cover:

1.  **New trend: open source projects overwhelmed by AI-generated security reports.** Projects like Node.js, Django, and Fastify are restricting or dropping vulnerability reporting platforms like HackerOne.
    
2.  **AI agents keep changing engineers’ working styles.** Four cases of tech professionals changing how they work thanks to AI agents – including Uncle Bob Martin (author of _“Clean Code”_) who might entertain the possibility that code readability matters less with AI.
    
3.  **Layoffs at big, profitable companies.** Amazon cuts 16,000 corporate jobs and Pinterest lays off 15% of staff, despite the businesses being profitable. Both sets of job losses seem “quarterly-driven” and timed before earnings announcements.
    
4.  **“AI-native” techies increasingly in-demand: two stories.** A product manager who spent 2,500 hours building with AI agents finally landed his dream engineering job specifically because he’s “AI-native.” Meanwhile, a startup failed to hire a junior AI engineer in two months of searching.
    
5.  **Industry Pulse.** Claude Code installs exploded in January, OpenAI acqui-hired most of the Cline team, Anthropic forces Clawdbot rebrand, GitHub is finally fixing its slow UI, China’s Kimi K2.5 matches Opus 4.5 for a fraction of the cost, and one AI code review vendor questions whether the space is in a bubble.
