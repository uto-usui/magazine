## Iconography

Functional icons are derived from the language of the mark: rounded corners with flat caps. They are set in a 192px grid at 16px thickness, meaning at 16x16px they use a 1.33px thickness. At 12x12px, stroke thickness isn't scaled down, rather forms are reduced and simplified.
