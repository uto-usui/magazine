[Ian](https://ianfrostweather.com/), [TJ](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tpitre/), and I are excited to announce **live AI & Design Systems Jam Sessions** with our [AI & Design Systems](https://aianddesign.systems/) course community! **Our first jam session will be Thursday, February 26 at 10AM ET.**

In these recurring biweekly Zoom sessions, we’ll show off the latest and greatest demos and material from [our emerging course](https://aianddesign.systems/?utm_source=Brad+Frost&utm_campaign=9a6592f5a8-EMAIL_CAMPAIGN_2025_12_30_09_00_COPY_02&utm_medium=email&utm_term=0_-2bdfafa15b-&mc_cid=9a6592f5a8&mc_eid=UNIQID), talk shop, answer questions, and give people the opportunity to show off what they’re up to. This is an opportunity to get connected, get inspired, get unstuck, get feedback, swap notes, and nerd out with other passionate practitioners.

It’s abundantly clear that this landscape is constantly evolving, which is why we’re taking a more communal approach to creating this course. We’re still making steady progress on the actual course videos; in fact, **we just added videos framing the whole course and shared our principles and approach to working with AI**. These live sessions will augment the videos, allowing us to both share our latest and greatest as well and to learn from the community to influence the course.

**This live session is exclusive to people who [preorder our AI & Design Systems course](https://aianddesign.systems/#order)**, so tell your friends/colleagues to preorder if they want to party with us!
