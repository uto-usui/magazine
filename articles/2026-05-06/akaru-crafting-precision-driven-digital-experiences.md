## Showcase

Below are a few projects that reflect how we approach digital design, storytelling, and performance.

### Sanctuary on the Moon: A Digital Journey Beyond Earth

With Sanctuary, we explored an ambitious vision: preserving human knowledge beyond our planet through sapphire discs sent to the Moon. For us, this project was an opportunity to create a deeply immersive and conceptual digital experience, blending storytelling, science, and speculative design.

We designed a journey that invites users into a poetic space between technology and philosophy, using motion, transitions, and spatial composition to convey the scale and symbolism of the project. The challenge was not only technical, but narrative: translating an abstract and visionary concept into an experience that feels both accessible and meaningful.

The result is a platform somewhere between interactive storytelling and digital installation, pushing the boundaries of what a website can express.

### Archidomo: Elevating Real Estate Through Digital Experience

With Archidomo, we reimagined how real estate can be experienced online. Rather than simply listing properties, we created a platform where each space becomes a narrative. We designed an interface that highlights architecture, materials, and atmosphere through refined visuals, editorial layouts, smooth transitions, and carefully paced interactions.

Our challenge was to balance aesthetic sophistication with usability, delivering a premium, almost cinematic experience without compromising clarity.

### Vaonis: Reimagining the Telescope Experience

For Vaonis, we designed an e-commerce platform capable of translating the wonder of space exploration into a compelling digital journey. We combined immersive visuals with a structured user experience, guiding visitors from discovery to purchase while showcasing the beauty of astrophotography.

The challenge was clear: balancing emotion and conversion. We created an experience that inspires while remaining fluid and efficient.
