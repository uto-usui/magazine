On rounded forms

A pebble acquires its shape through contact: with water, with grit, with other stones. Its edges soften until something hard becomes inviting to hold. The objects gathered here bring that familiarity into manufactured form, from things carried in a pocket to a place to sit.

![A dark oval pebble against a black background, with light catching its curved edge and revealing its finely textured surface.](https://mouthprint.com/media/pages/e002/50a48c4a2c-1791558002/minorstep_pebble.webp)
