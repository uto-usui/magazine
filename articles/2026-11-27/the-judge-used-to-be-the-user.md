## Someone remembered the old way

A stranger on X mentioned my name three days ago. He had worked near me years ago, when I was an AI research engineer at an Indonesian e-commerce unicorn, and he said I was good at it. I now work somewhere else. The thread was about something unrelated.

I opened my contacts and messaged old colleagues. Reading the praise, I kept thinking it belonged to them. They were the ones who were better than me, and most of what I knew I had learned sitting next to them.

Then I started thinking about how that work used to feel. Heavy, slow, and a lot of fun. And I wondered whether building a model by hand is about to become the film photography of our field.

The comparison is tempting. Phones made film impractical, and then film came back anyway. Kodak says [consumer demand for film doubled in five years](https://www.digitalcameraworld.com/cameras/film-cameras/kodak-shares-surprising-statistics-on-the-rise-of-film-as-factory-reopens-after-shutdown). An estimated 42M people now shoot on film, and [a third of them are between 18 and 30](https://www.ypulse.com/?p=184993), too young to remember when it was the only option.

Few of them shoot film to get a sharper picture. A roll has 36 frames, so you look before you press. Then you wait for the lab…
