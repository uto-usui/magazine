These are great questions, and questions that are answered _in depth_ in our [Atomic Design](https://atomicdesigncourse.com/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=web&utm_campaign=bf2026&utm_content=blog-1) and [Subatomic Design Tokens](https://designtokenscourse.com/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=web&utm_campaign=bf2026&utm_content=blog-1) courses.
