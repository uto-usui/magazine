What is Mean for?

Anything where pointing beats describing: design and motion review, bug reports and QA, product walkthroughs. Point to the spacing that's off, jump to the frame where something broke, then share the annotations—or hand them to a coding agent as a visual prompt.

Why does it need Screen Recording permission?

Used to read your screen when you capture.

Where do my captures go?

To History on your Mac, and to your Mean account on the web when you sign in.

What does the export look like?

Two modes. Text copies a one-line command — paste it to an agent and it reads the whole session. Image copies one picture with the comments joined on.

What is a .mean file?

Mean's session format — a portable, versioned archive of one session: images and comments together. Reopen it and keep annotating.

Is it free?

Yes. Mean is free to download and use, distributed as a DMG from this site.

Where do I ask questions?

In the [Discord](https://discord.mean.app/) — for questions and bug reports.
