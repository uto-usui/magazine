### Graphic System

Simple rules for infinite possibilities

We built a system that turns complexity into possibility. A few recognizable ingredients — color, modules and layers — combine and recombine to create range and flexibility. Motion extends the same logic, bringing the system to life from bold brand moments to animated illustrations and interactions inside the app. Together, they give Nubank room to grow in every direction.

### Art Direction

Put life in the picture

The real benefit of effortless banking is spending less time thinking about money. So we focused on everything that leaves room for: people out in the world, absorbed in living. Real relationships and unguarded moments of joy. Photography makes financial freedom feel less like a promise from a bank, and more like a life being lived.

### Illustration

Room to imagine more

Illustration gives Nubank another way to make ideas feel human, bringing warmth, imagination and play to stories that can be hard to capture in the real world. Designed to move as well as stand still, it stretches from bold brand moments to the smallest interactions inside the app, giving Nubank another way to inform and connect.

### Digital Design

From launch to login

For a digital-first bank, simplicity at global scale takes serious work. We brought the new brand to [nu.com](http://nu.com/), designing a website that could work across products, languages and regulatory requirements while still feeling effortless to use, keeping the complexity behind the scenes and possibility front and center.

### Croma

Growing with you

Croma is built for customers who have grown with Nubank and now want more from their bank, bringing together enhanced credit, rewards and everyday benefits for the next stage of their financial lives. Its identity feels just as progressive: more expressive and colorful, while staying rooted in the familiar ingredients that make Nubank unique.

### Ultravioleta

Wealth on new terms

Ultravioleta is Nubank’s premium offering, built for customers with more sophisticated financial needs. Its identity takes Nubank somewhere richer and more refined, stretching color, imagery and voice into a world of its own, without falling back on the traditional codes of exclusivity that have long defined premium banking.

Taking Nu to the world

### Outcome

Nubank’s never stood still, and now the brand’s built to move with it: one system with the flexibility to stretch across new markets, products and audiences, and the clarity to keep everything connected. So wherever Nubank goes next, it can stay true to the idea that made it different: making money feel effortless.
