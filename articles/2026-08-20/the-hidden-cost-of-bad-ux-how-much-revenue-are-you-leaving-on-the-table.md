## How the Opportunity Sizing Tool Calculates Recoverable Revenue

The calculator projects potential conversion lifts by applying parameters derived from **200,000+ hours of UX testing** across 40+ ecommerce industries.

### Business Performance Inputs

To generate a baseline projection, the calculator evaluates your site’s monthly traffic alongside **one of two** standard performance metrics:

-   **Option A:** Revenue per Visitor (RPV)
-   **Option B:** Average Order Value (AOV) combined with Conversion Rate (CVR)

The tool also takes into account your industry to **anchor the calculations** to relevant [benchmark](https://baymard.com/product/ux-benchmarking) data.

### Standardized Guideline Scoring

Every [UX best practice guideline](https://baymard.com/product/ux-best-practice-guidelines) in Baymard’s catalog carries an estimated lift based on **two core factors**:

1.  **Frequency of encounter:** The percentage of users who interact with the problematic UI component
2.  **Severity of friction:** The conversion impact observed during user testing when a site violates the guideline

Quantifying the individual guideline allows us to **quantify the opportunity size** for each UX theme, and for each Baymard subscription plan.

That means we can help you decide on the best plan for your team and organization, and **show the ROI you can expect** from implementing best practices available within that plan.

### Compounding Fixes with Diminishing Returns

Fixing two separate UX issues on a website **does not** produce simple additive results.

For example, resolving an issue in the cart improves the baseline performance of the site, and any subsequent fix in the checkout flow operates on that **newly improved baseline**.

The calculator applies a compounding model to reflect diminishing returns, ensuring that combined projections **remain mathematically grounded** rather than artificially stacked.

### Applied Revenue Caps

Even a complete overhaul of every UX issue **will not double** a site’s conversion rate overnight. There is a practical ceiling to the impact of usability improvements alone.

To maintain realistic financial projections, the methodology applies standard cap models to total revenue lift, **preventing** the opportunity size from being overinflated:

-   **Conservative (low):** 15% revenue lift cap
-   **Baseline (midpoint):** 25% revenue lift cap
-   **Aggressive (high):** 35% revenue lift cap
