## Handle every agreement from Claude

The Lumin connector lets you create agreements from a chat or template and send them for eSignatures without leaving Claude. For agreements you receive, review terms against past agreements before you sign.

## Your AI-powered agreement workflow

### Consistency made simple

Agreements built from your Lumin templates keep your approved wording every time. Draft one-off deals with Claude, then save the ones that work as templates for next time.

### Get agreements sent sooner

Send the agreement as soon as the terms are settled, while the deal is still moving. Keeping it in one chat leaves less room for delays.

## Your agreement workflow in Claude

Create, send and track every agreement in Claude. Fewer tools and fewer handoffs.

-   ![](https://images.prismic.io/lumin-pdf/fq0Wzs7RzbcQNf0a_Sales-profile.png?auto=format,compress)
    
    ### Build agreements your way
    
    Draft the agreement with Claude from your notes, start from a Lumin template or upload a file from a link. Then save it as a template to reuse on the next deal.
    
-   ![](https://images.prismic.io/lumin-pdf/agVlg3JX22FO7RuS_Property-profile.png?auto=format,compress)
    
    ### Send for signature from the conversation
    
    Tell Claude who needs to sign and by when. It creates the signature request in Lumin and checks with you before anything goes out.
    
    [Try in Claude](https://claude.ai/new?q=Create%20a%2012-month%20residential%20lease%20for%20Unit%204B%20at%20%242%2C100%20a%20month%2C%20starting%20December%201.%20Send%20it%20to%20%5Btenant%201%20email%5D%20and%20%5Btenant%202%20email%5D%20to%20sign.)
    
-   ![](https://images.prismic.io/lumin-pdf/kfZ-IcXFwCuNyl5W_SaaS-profile.png?auto=format,compress)
    
    ### Check an agreement's status
    
    Ask “Who still hasn’t signed?” and Claude pulls the latest status. You see which signers have completed it and when and each signature is logged in an audit trail.
    
-   ![](https://images.prismic.io/lumin-pdf/hiqZdmN5pXBfDmYE_Construction-profile.png?auto=format,compress)
    
    ### Works wherever you use Claude
    
    Use the connector in Claude on web, desktop and mobile, or in Claude Code. Connect it once and it's ready in every chat.
    

## How to get started with Lumin for Claude

-   ![](https://images.prismic.io/lumin-pdf/2cOSQiKpeLZM6vVR_Getting-started-cards_01.png?auto=format,compress)
    
    ### Step 1
    
    In Claude, go to Customize > Connectors and find Lumin.
    
-   ![](https://images.prismic.io/lumin-pdf/oNOk4vTtVN82eIxJ_Getting-started-cards_02.png?auto=format,compress)
    
    ### Step 2
    
    Sign in to your Lumin account or create one. Claude then works inside your workspace with your permissions.
    
-   ![](https://images.prismic.io/lumin-pdf/zJW_OPfoOqeqvAkl_Getting-started-cards_03.png?auto=format,compress)
    
    ### Step 3
    
    Start from a chat or a template and Claude creates the agreement. Review it, then send it for eSignatures when you're ready.
