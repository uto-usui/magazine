You can now transfer Marketplace resources between teams directly from the Vercel dashboard without relying on the API. This simplifies resource management during team or project changes. Both owner and member roles on the source and destination teams can initiate transfers.

The destination team must have the corresponding integration installed before receiving a resource. The feature currently supports transfer databases from [Prisma](https://vercel.com/marketplace/prisma), [Neon](https://vercel.com/marketplace/neon) and [Supabase](https://vercel.com/marketplace/supabase) , with additional providers and product support coming soon.

Start from your database settings in the dashboard, or learn more in the [documentation](http://vercel.com/docs/integrations/install-an-integration/product-integration#transfer-a-resource-to-another-team).
