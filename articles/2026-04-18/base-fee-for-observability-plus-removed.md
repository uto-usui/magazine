We've eliminated the $10 base fee for Observability Plus for all customers. We now only charge you for the observability events collected.

[Observability Plus](https://vercel.com/docs/observability/observability-plus) gives you full visibility into your Vercel data with enhanced logs and metrics, anomaly alerts, 30-day log retention, and the context to debug issues fast.

[Start using Observability Plus](https://vercel.com/d?to=%2F%5Bteam%5D%2F%7E%2Fobservability) today.
