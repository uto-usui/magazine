Mar 16, 2026

The Vercel AI Accelerator is back, and this year we selected 39 early-stage teams from across the US, Europe, Asia, and Latin America to build with us for six weeks.

The next generation of AI startups is building on our [self-driving infrastructure](https://vercel.com/blog/self-driving-infrastructure), and the accelerator is how we work directly with the earliest-stage founders among them. This year’s cohort spans every industry, at varying points in their journey, but they share a clear point of view on what needs to exist right now and the urgency to ship it.

Teams in the program get access to Vercel infrastructure, [$8M+ in partner credits](https://vercel.com/blog/the-vercel-ai-accelerator-is-back-with-6-million-in-credits) (AWS, Anthropic, OpenAI, Cartesia, ElevenLabs, and more), weekly sessions with founders and technical leaders, and dedicated VC mentors.

## [Link to heading](#what's-next-for-the-cohort)What's next for the cohort

On March 18th, we're bringing the cohort together with the entire Vercel ecosystem for Builder Day at our new San Francisco headquarters. The day will include a fireside chat with Guillermo Rauch (Vercel CEO) and Stephen Haney ([Paper](https://paper.design/) CEO), as well as office hours with our accelerator partners.

From there, teams will have four weeks to refine and ship, leading up to Demo Day on April 16th in San Francisco. Each team will present what they've built to AI leaders, VCs, and builders, and judges will select three winners to receive over $100K in credits and prizes from Vercel and our partners, including an investment from Vercel Ventures for the first-place team.

Past accelerator teams like Stably AI, Cervo, and Bear AI have gone on to raise venture rounds, land enterprise customers, and form partnerships through the program. Follow along as this year's cohort builds over the next six weeks.
