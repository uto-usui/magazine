You can now run cron jobs from your application in the summary section of your deployments dashboard.

Try it out by deploying a [Vercel cron job template](https://vercel.com/templates/template/vercel-cron). Once you deploy, Vercel automatically registers your cron jobs.

Learn more in the [cron jobs documentation](https://vercel.com/docs/cron-jobs).
