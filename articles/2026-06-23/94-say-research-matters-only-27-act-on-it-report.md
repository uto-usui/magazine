## About the report

Most companies say they listen to their customers. The truth is more complicated. In The State of Modern Research 2026: Closing the Listening Gap, we surveyed 309 researchers and research leaders to find out why.

We discovered a major contradiction: companies are collecting more feedback than ever, but customer insights don’t consistently make it into their business decisions. That’s the listening gap.  
‍  
**Four trends widening the listening gap**:

1.  Research has scaled. The supporting systems haven't kept up.
2.  Senior leaders and practitioners disagree about the problem. They're both right.
3.  Most researchers use AI. Few are getting the most from it.
4.  Organizations want democratized research. But they don't buy tools everyone can use.
