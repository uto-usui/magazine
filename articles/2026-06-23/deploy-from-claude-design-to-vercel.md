Vercel is now a send-to destination in [Claude Design](https://claude.ai/design). When you finish a design, you can send it to Vercel and get a live URL back without leaving your canvas.

Claude Design deploys the design as a new project in your connected Vercel account and returns a URL you can open and share.

When you're ready to share a design, add Vercel as your destination in the 'Share' menu and connect the [Vercel MCP server](https://vercel.com/docs/mcp/vercel-mcp) to get started.

[Learn more](https://vercel.com/kb/guide/claude-design) about using Claude Design and Vercel together.
