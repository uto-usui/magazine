The Idea Machine is a playful generator that combines three things: a [space in human life](https://www.the-idea-machine.com/maps/space), a [product form](https://www.the-idea-machine.com/maps/format), and an [unexpected twist](https://www.the-idea-machine.com/maps/twist).

Pull the lever to discover one of more than 1,000,000 possible ideas.

Built on a simple belief: good ideas often begin with an unlikely combination — and a little serendipity.
