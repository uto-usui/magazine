A very handy looking, _tiny_ progressive web components library.

A beautifully composed piece that should serve as a nice reminder to you of both how much “smart” software/devices dominate your time and also how it’s not your fault!

Does exactly what it says on the tin!

A bit like how you should avoid prefixing `alt` text with “an image”, prefixing/suffixing `<nav>` labels with “navigation” should be avoided too.

Outrageously impressive stuff here! It plays surprisingly well and smoothly too.
