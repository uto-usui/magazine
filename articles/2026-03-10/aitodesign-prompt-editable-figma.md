## Generate designs from prompts

ai.to.design uses AI to create editable Figma design.

Ask anything!

## Supported AI models

1.  GPT by OpenAI
2.  Gemini by Google
3.  Claude by Anthropic
4.  Grok by xAI
5.  GLM by zAi
6.  Kimi AI by MoonshotAI
7.  DeepSeek
8.  MiniMax

## How to generate designs from prompts?

1.  In Figma, go to the plugin menu and run this plugin ai.to.design
2.  Select the AI model you want to use
3.  Enter your text prompt
4.  Let AI generate the designs for you right on your canvas
