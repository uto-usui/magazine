## FAQ

What are the benefits of Flodesk Studio?

Flodesk Studio is an AI design app that helps small business owners create beautiful, on-brand emails in seconds, with full control over every pixel. Six core benefits:

-   **Hours become seconds.** Less template-picking, less trying to fit layouts and content together, less blinking cursor. Studio gets you 80% of the way there in a single prompt, so you can focus on the fun, creative part.
-   **A head start, every time.** Begin from some copy, a prompt, or an idea in your head. No brand yet? Start from a gorgeous Flodesk preset and add your custom touches.
-   **Emails that are unmistakably yours.** Upload your fonts, colors, images, voice and tone, and business context. Then let Studio make it magic.
-   **Edit your way.** Refine via chat or fine-tune by hand. You stay in control the whole way through, with hundreds of new layouts to choose from.
-   **Works with any email provider (but best with Flodesk, of course).** Take your design anywhere that supports HTML email. You shouldn't have to switch platforms for beautiful emails.
-   **Built with you.** Email is just the start. Forms, pages, and more are on the way, based directly on your feedback. Studio is a human-created design system, accelerated by AI, perfected by you.

What is Flodesk Studio?

Flodesk Studio is an AI email design app that turns a simple description into a beautiful, on-brand email in seconds. It starts with real design work from world-class designers, accelerated by AI and finished by you. Create in Studio and send from Flodesk, or export your design to any platform that supports HTML email.

What's the difference between Flodesk and Flodesk Studio?

Flodesk is the full email marketing platform, where you build your list, send emails, and grow your business. Flodesk Studio is a separate app focused on one thing: designing beautiful, on-brand emails with AI. You can design in Studio and send through Flodesk in one click, or export your design to another platform. Think of Flodesk as where you send, and Studio as where you design.

Is Flodesk Studio free?

Yes. Studio is free to use while it's in beta, with no credit card required. Bring your brand, make a few emails, and share your honest feedback as we keep building it.

Can I use Flodesk Studio with other email platforms?

Yes. You can export your Studio design to any email platform that supports HTML email, so you're never locked in. If you're a Flodesk member, you can also send your design in one click without leaving Flodesk.

Do I need design experience to use Flodesk Studio?

No. Studio is built for small business owners and creators, not designers. It starts you from a professionally designed foundation and applies your brand automatically, so you get a polished email even if you've never designed one before.

Does Flodesk Studio use AI to create my emails?

Studio uses AI, but not the way most tools do. Every design begins with real work from world-class designers, then AI applies your brand and speeds things up, and you stay in control through every edit. The result looks designed, not generated.

What can I make in Flodesk Studio right now?

Right now, Studio designs email, beautifully and on-brand. Forms, pages, and more marketing assets are on the way, each one built with the same care. Studio is just getting started, and we're building the rest in public.

Will Flodesk Studio keep my information safe?

Yes, always. Studio is a separate app, and it doesn't have access to your existing content, data or subscribers' information unless you explicitly give it access. It's powered by Claude (made by Anthropic) for now, and designed to grow with the best AI out there over time. Whatever you make in Studio belongs to you, start to finish.
