Last year's first-ever State of Devs survey covered career, workplace, health, and even worldview issues that are often left unadressed in the developer community.

And it only made sense to follow up with this second edition about all the aspects of a developer's life that are _not_ code, especially at a time where AI has started disrupting the very nature of our jobs.

So while there's still a human in the loop, let's leave aside libraries and frameworks and just check in: how are you doing, _really_?

As usual, every question is optional, so feel free to skip any that you don't feel comfortable answering.
