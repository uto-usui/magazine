📙 News, Opinions & Tutorials

[![](https://res.cloudinary.com/cpress/image/upload/w_1280,e_sharpen:60,q_auto/v1779195604/ujztdjxqv8vnqvnpjzlt.png)](https://ishadeed.com/article/css-round/)

[Better Fluid Sizing with `round()`](https://ishadeed.com/article/css-round/ "ishadeed.com") — Ahmad, in his typically accessible style, writes about how the well-supported [CSS `round()` function](https://webstatus.dev/features/round-mod-rem) can now be used to help create predictable fluid sizing. He notes how it pairs well with `clamp()` to assist on sizing, typography, and spacing. The result? Cleaner and easier to maintain type scales, along with refined responsive layouts overall.

Ahmad Shadeed

[Gap Decorations: Now Available in Chromium](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/gap-decorations-stable?hl=en "developer.chrome.com") — Imminent versions of both Chrome and Edge will support CSS gap decorations — a nice way to easily style the gaps found between grid, flexbox and multi-column layouts, without the need for pseudo-elements or borders.
