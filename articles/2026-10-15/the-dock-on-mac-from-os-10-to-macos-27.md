I wrote [the chapter on app icon history for _The iOS App Icon Book_](https://blog.jim-nielsen.com/2022/short-history-of-ios-app-icons/).

The other day I found an old note stashed away where I had started researching the macOS version of that chapter (which Michael ultimately ended up writing).

At the time, I didn’t know what to write. I mean, how do you write a history of icon design for the Mac? It’s rich!

To get my creative juices flowing, I started by researching the different docks in macOS through the years and compiled them all into a single graphic (big shout out to [this compilation of images on 512 Pixels](https://512pixels.net/projects/aqua-screenshot-library/compare/)).

![Screenshot of mac app icons as they appeared in the dock from OSX Cheetah to macOS Golden Gate](https://cdn.jim-nielsen.com/blog/2026/macos-dock-history.jpg)

I just wanted to preserve and archive that image. Check out [the high-res version here](https://cdn.jim-nielsen.com/blog/2026/macos-dock-history-lg.png).

That’s it, that’s the post.
