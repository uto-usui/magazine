[Drama 3 from Fish Audio](https://vercel.com/ai-gateway/models/drama-3) is now available on AI Gateway, and 80% off for 30 days (through Nov 12).

The text-to-speech model is currently in preview and lets you direct voice performances with natural-language instructions in your script, making it useful for voiceovers, character dialogue, and audio storytelling.

Describe the emotion and pacing in square brackets, add sounds and pauses where they belong, or wrap selected words in tags such as `<whisper>` and `<emphasis>` to change their delivery. Directions can be written in a different language from the spoken text. Drama 3 also supports multi-speaker dialogue, with a separate voice for each character.

Use `fish-audio/drama-3` as the model name to get started:

Try Drama 3 in the [model playground](https://vercel.com/ai-gateway/models/drama-3).

AI Gateway provides a unified API for calling models, tracking usage and cost, and configuring retries, failover, and performance optimizations for higher-than-provider uptime. It includes built-in [custom reporting](https://vercel.com/changelog/custom-reporting-ai-gateway), [Zero Data Retention support](https://vercel.com/blog/zdr-on-ai-gateway), [budgets for API keys](https://vercel.com/docs/ai-gateway/authentication-and-byok/api-keys), [routing rules](https://vercel.com/docs/ai-gateway/models-and-providers/routing-rules), and more.

AI Gateway reflects provider pricing with no markup and does not charge a platform fee on inference, including on [Bring Your Own Key](https://vercel.com/docs/ai-gateway/authentication-and-byok/byok) (BYOK) requests.
