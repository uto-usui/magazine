On the latest episode of [Wake Up Excited!](https://wakeupexcited.show/), I got to talk with my friend, fellow traveler, and recent collaborator, [Ben Callahan](https://bencallahan.com/). Ben and I met at [BDConf](https://bradfrost.com/blog/post/bdconf-mobilewood-10-years-later/) many many moons ago, and since then we’ve shared many adventures together, including recently collaborating on an actual conversation. I have so much admiration for Ben and the community-oriented way he operates, so I’m incredibly pleased we got to spend a whole conversation digging deep into it.

We cover so much ground in our conversation: building healthy communities, the critical importance of values, humanizing work, how hard things force you to reevaluate things, the sharing culture of the web, lessons from music, the cosmos, and a whole lot more.
