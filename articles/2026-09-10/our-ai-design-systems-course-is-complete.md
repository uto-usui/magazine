## The arc of the course

It was a LOT of work just even to arrive at a structure for the course, and we're really proud of how we're covering AI & Design Systems:

1.  **Core concepts.** The benefits & challenges of design systems, the great potential (and great risks!) of AI, and how AI & design systems together make a powerful combo.
2.  **Environments & tools setup.** We demystify installing and configuring LLMs, AI-powered IDEs, Figma, Node, API keys, MCPs, Git & GitHub, and more.
3.  **Make better design systems with AI.** Make your design system AI-ready and address your check engine light. Learn how to use AI to help audit, improve, and synchronize your design system assets.
4.  **Make better products with AI & design systems.** Apply AI & design systems together across the entire product design & development process to unlock quality, consistency, efficiency, and scale.
5.  **Inventing the next generation of UX.** Inventing the future! Agents as users, generative UI, hyper-personalization, real-time interfaces, and the creative infinite.
6.  **Operationalizing AI & design systems at your organization.** Get buy-in, run a pilot program, roll out across teams, and establish the governance to help your org navigate this fast-moving landscape.

We're well aware that this landscape is changing literally every day, which is why we've designed this course to be very much alive! We've been careful to present durable core concepts while leaving the door open for ongoing lessons and demos as the landscape continues to evolve.
