* * *

Twenty twenty six, what an uneventful year, eh?

That’s sarcasm. The entire year has been nothing but incessant chatbot addicts toking their token pipes and shouting: _“Everything has changed! Don’t get left behind!”_

Despite regularly being mistaken for a software “engineer” I am but a humble website developer. And by “website developer” I mean _website developer_, not [React developer](https://jsx.lol/); very different. Not that anyone understands this anymore. I’ll be lambasted for making fallacious [“no true Scotsman”](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_true_Scotsman) arguments for asserting that real web dev has actual _standards_.

Nevertheless, today I’m talking about/to the real web industry.

What’s left of it, anyway.

## The web industry

TL;DR: the web industry is dismantling itself from the inside.

Events of the last year have been incomprehensibly stupid. The rapid deskilling and decimation of professional talent is a shock to the system. It’s demoralising to see once-respected peers drop all pretence of care for their profession.

Every week I sigh _“et tu, Brute?”_ as I strike off another peer from my Christmas card list.

Web professionals are cashing in their credibility to shill for the [AI industrial complex](https://dbushell.com/ai/). People who have spent years, decades in some cases, building a reputation as leaders within the web industry are now destroying what they’ve built. They step on the heads of those who are fighting to stay afloat. I don’t need to name names, they won’t shut up about their chatbots. You know the type. They gush AI platitudes as if the singularity is holding a gun to their head. And they’ve solved everything! Web development? Chatbot. Web design? Chatbot. The websites they’re building? Believe it or not; chatbots.

And what has the web industry actually gained after all this performative slop?

-   Entry level jobs have evaporated
-   Web development education is in the gutter
-   Web infrastructure is getting hammered

It’s embarrassing how much the web industry has lost in so little time. It has cannibalised itself and those responsible seem oblivious to the cause and effect of their actions.

Let’s get one thing absolutely clear: [there is no career in prompting](https://dbushell.com/2026/09/28/leaving-them-behind/). There is no scenario where replacing your work with a chatbot will have a long-term positive impact on your career. Unless you’re into S&M at the beck and call of billionaires. I mean, I don’t want to kink shame, but keep it out off the workplace, please!

The [“it’s just a tool”](https://dbushell.com/2026/04/28/alternative-thoughts/#its-just-a-tool) and [“inevitable”](https://dbushell.com/2026/01/26/ai-policy-and-the-inevitable/#ai-inevitabilism) rhetoric doesn’t cut it. For heaven’s sake, even if you _continue_ to ignore [all other moral an ethical issues](https://dbushell.com/ai/#rationale), at least open your eyes to the absurdity of what’s being sold here. **Tokens are not the commodity, you are.**

Replacing professionally trained skills with [markdown files](https://dbushell.com/2026/05/20/google-just-spat-in-my-face/) and ceding control to chatbot subscriptions is not a sustainable future for the web industry. I’m not here to debate whether chatbot-driven web design/development is “good enough”. Although I’ll note that many who claim this are confusing [“taste”](https://dbushell.com/2026/09/04/abattoirs-of-taste/) with the _trained talent_ they’ve driven out of town.

What possible _positive_ future are we expecting by locking knowledge and workflow behind a chatbot subscription? How is this even a question that needs asking?

To be honest, it’s hard to blame those who’ve [decided to give up](https://dbushell.com/2026/04/01/i-quit-the-clankers-won/) and ride chatbots all the way down. It’s certainly easier than remaining optimistic about the web industry in face of the grifters who dominate the conversation. You must know one thing though: the AI boosters in our industry are a vocal minority. Those suffering to maintain sanity are silenced by AI mandates and fear of repercussions. Not me though. I’m in a position to call out this bullshit. I do so because every week I get heartbreaking emails and private DMs from those suffering. That’s why I write what I write. People are hurting. You’re not alone people!

I encourage everyone to look outside the bubble and realise that the rest of humanity thinks “AI” is the dumbest shit the tech industry has ever shat.

Do not allow your brain to atrophy just because “everyone else is doing it.”

If you’ve lost respect for the web, if you’re destroying the web on behalf of gatekeepers like Google — there’s a topic for another post — do the rest of us a favour and _leave_. If you haven’t given up, _tell people._ We need more voices speaking up against this nonsense.

I’m not giving up. I’ll continue to fight for a human web.
