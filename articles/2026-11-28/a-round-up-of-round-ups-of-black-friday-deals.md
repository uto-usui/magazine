The independent education community are really coming together this year to give each other a foot-up. It’s in response to [the bad times](https://bell.bz/in-response-to-the-death-of-web-development-education/) we’ve gone through and we’ve ended up becoming a nice, tight community.

We’re all determined to push on in 2027 and give you, the readers, the good stuff as we navigate more and more change in our industry. The tighter the industry community is, the better for everyone. Us independent educators and publishers [won’t stop](https://piccalil.li/blog/if-we-stop-we-lose/) and will try to be at the centre of that.

Traditionally, we’ve done a round up of Black Friday deals on Black Friday itself here, but this year, we’re mixing it up a bit. We’re rounding up the other round-ups. The hope is to give as many as possible a signal boost at what is the [most important time of year](https://piccalil.li/links/black-friday-2026-is-live/).

If you can, support independents so we can all move into 2027 with a boost. We all win then 💛
