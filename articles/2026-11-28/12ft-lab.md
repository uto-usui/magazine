## FAQ

Are the tools free to use?

Yes, completely. Everything runs right in your browser, without your data and without AI. When you download or export something, we ask whether you would like to donate. You don't have to, but it helps us keep the projects going.

Can I use the fonts commercially?

Yes, use them for whatever you like, and feel free to build on them. We'd be glad about a small donation after the download, so we can keep making things like this, and about a mention. Neither is required.

Who is behind 12ft Lab?

[12ft.studio](https://12ft.studio/). This is where we collect the experiments, tools and prototypes that come about alongside our projects. Your contact is Philipp York ([Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/philippyork/)).

Do you build tools for clients?

We do, and many of our tools are already in use. Just get in touch through our [contact page](https://12ft.studio/en/kontakt).

How do I report a bug?

First of all, thanks for asking: feedback is everything. The easiest way is to connect with [@philippyork on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/philippyork/) and send him a message. If you'd rather not use Instagram, reach us through the [contact page on 12ft.studio](https://12ft.studio/en/kontakt). We appreciate your help 🐛❤️

Will there be more?

We can't say when or what exactly: we do this alongside our client work, and every project is tested before it goes live, which takes time. But we definitely want to keep this going, and for that we rely on your feedback, so let us know what you'd like to see.
