## It started  
in the stores.

Bodega Cats of New York began with visits to neighborhood stores. We asked about the cats and recorded what the workers and regulars told us.

The work has grown into a book about 60 cats and a campaign for their protection.

[The story of BCNY](https://bodegacatsofnewyork.com/about/)
