[Announcing _Interop 2026_](https://frontendfoc.us/link/180841/web "webkit.org") — The [Interop project](https://frontendfoc.us/link/180842/web) forms the basis for Google, Apple, Igalia, Microsoft, and Mozilla to collaborate each year on implementing the web technology that developers most want and need. This year sees _fifteen_ brand new topics, including improvements to `attr()`, container style queries, `contrast-color()`, scroll-driven animations, and tightening up the CSS Scroll Snap spec.

Wu and Simmons (WebKit)

💡 Each of the partners has announced Interop 2026 and explained their role in it separately, [here's Google's post by Rachel Andrew](https://frontendfoc.us/link/180843/web), [Jack Archibald writing for Mozilla](https://frontendfoc.us/link/180844/web), [Microsoft's take](https://frontendfoc.us/link/180845/web), and [Igalia's roundup.](https://frontendfoc.us/link/180846/web) We felt WebKit's post (above) _just_ led the way, though, as it includes more code examples! :-)

⚡️ IN BRIEF

-   🥳 [Chrome 145 has been released](https://frontendfoc.us/link/180848/web) with support for `column-wrap` and `column-height` properties – [demos and explanations here.](https://frontendfoc.us/link/180849/web) The DevTools have [also seen some enhancements.](https://frontendfoc.us/link/180850/web)
    
-   🕹️ Not content to merely [port Quake to run in the browser](https://frontendfoc.us/link/180851/web), the creator of Three.js now has [a _Descent_ port too](https://frontendfoc.us/link/180852/web) ([source](https://frontendfoc.us/link/180853/web)).
    
-   ✋ The EU wants [to tackle 'addictive UI' patterns](https://frontendfoc.us/link/180854/web) like infinite scrolling.
    
-   🤖 [Google shows off a preview of WebMCP](https://frontendfoc.us/link/180855/web), a proposed standard for sites to offer up abilities to AI agents.
    
-   🤖 Cloudflare is rolling out [a feature to allow agents to fetch Markdown directly](https://frontendfoc.us/link/180856/web) from Cloudflare-powered sites.
    
-   📄 The CSSWG has published a first [public working draft of Selectors Level 5.](https://frontendfoc.us/link/180857/web)
