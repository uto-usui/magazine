Everyone's Interop 2027 Wishlists

Last week we shared that you can now [rank the Interop web platform proposals for 2027](https://interop-rank.fxdx.dev/). Since then, plenty of folks have shared their own rankings and explained their choices:

-   ⭐ [Josh Comeau's _The Future of CSS_](https://www.joshwcomeau.com/css/interop-2027/) – Josh's top ten, from CSS functions and `corner-shape` to anchored container queries and scroll-triggered animations, each with live demos, current browser support, and tips you can use today. A particularly good place to start.
    
-   🔎 [Sara Soueidan's picks](https://www.sarasoueidan.com/blog/interop-2027-rankings/) put accessibility first. It's a privilege, she notes, that we get to influence what browser makers work on. `focusgroup` is her top pick.
    
-   ✏️ [Declan Chidlow's 13 features](https://vale.rocks/micros/20261011-0700) – SVG improvements on top, followed by the CSS `reading-flow` and `reading-order` properties.
    
-   📝 More picks from [Stuart Robson](https://www.alwaystwisted.com/articles/and-thats-my-rank-2027-edition), [Eric Meyer](https://meyerweb.com/eric/thoughts/2026/10/14/my-interop-2027-rankings/), [Ana Rodrigues](https://ohhelloana.blog/my-interop-2027/), and [Tyler Sticka](https://tylersticka.com/journal/interop-2027-rank/), and 🦋 [this Bluesky thread](https://bsky.app/profile/sturobson.com/post/3mxlhrdlock22) has even more.
    

Inspired to submit your own? [Rank about ten proposals you'd like to see prioritized](https://interop-rank.fxdx.dev/). The Firefox team says that's enough to provide useful input.

[Safari Blocks 'Identity Resolution' Tracking Infrastructure](https://webkit.org/blog/18438/blocking-identity-resolution-infrastructure/ "webkit.org") — Cross-site cookies are long gone in Safari, so trackers moved to 'identity resolution' where third parties get the email address you type into forms and turn it into a cross-site, cross-device tracking ID. Safari now blocks them at the domain level.

⚡️ IN BRIEF

-   ⚠️ If PHP runs anywhere behind your sites: [PHP 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36 and 8.2.35 fix two critical bugs](https://www.php.net/archive/2026.php#2026-10-21-5) that allow unauthenticated remote code execution. Update now, _mod\_php_ installs especially.
    
-   🌐 [Chrome 157 is in beta](https://chromestatus.com/release-notes/157) with `corner` shorthands, a `<string>` value for `text-overflow`, and `anchor-valid`. Chrome 156 went stable this week with CSS `random()`.
    
-   🚫 [Chrome has finished deprecating the `unload` event](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/unload-deprecated-in-chrome): handlers no longer fire by default, so use `pagehide` or `visibilitychange` instead.
